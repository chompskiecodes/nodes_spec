# CP Physio & Health Solutions

## ABOUT THE CLINIC
### Overview
CP Physio provides physio, vestibular rehabilitation and concussion rehabilitation in Sydney's Inner West, near Sydney Park. The clinic is at St Peters. All consultations are 1:1 and hands-on, with functional rehabilitation focused on what you can do, and the team works with everyone involved in your care.
### Areas Treated
Vestibular rehabilitation (vertigo, dizziness, imbalance), concussion rehabilitation, acute and chronic neck and back pain, migraines, headaches and jaw pain, shoulder and elbow pain, knee pain, foot and ankle injuries, sporting injuries, post-op rehabilitation, work-related injuries and chronic pain conditions.

## SERVICES
### Musculoskeletal and Sports Physiotherapy
Assessment and treatment of any injury involving muscles, joints, ligaments or nerves, whether sustained at home, work, during recreational activity or sport.
### Vestibular Rehabilitation
Specialised assessment and treatment of vertigo (sensation of spinning), dizziness and imbalance. This may include BPPV repositioning techniques, other objective vestibular tests, and collaboration with GPs, ENT specialists and neurologists.
### Concussion Rehabilitation
There is more to managing a concussion than just rest, especially if rest has not helped settle the symptoms. The clinic creates a specific rehabilitation program to help you get back to school, work, sport and life.
### Head, Neck and Jaw Treatment
An effective treatment program that involves understanding the inter-relationship between your head, neck and jaw, in addition to other factors that may contribute to your presentation.
### Workcover and Work-Related Injuries
If you were injured at work and have lodged a Workcover claim, bring your claim number to your appointment. The clinic will discuss your progress with your case manager, return to work coordinator and doctor.
### Exercise Prescription
An essential part of rehabilitation. Exercise may involve a formal program for the gym or home, or simple changes to your daily routine.
### Dry Needling
Dry needling may be used as part of a physiotherapy treatment to help alleviate pain and improve function from a musculoskeletal injury.

## APPOINTMENT TYPES AND DURATIONS
#### Initial Consultation (50 minutes)
For a first visit, the time is used to identify your problem, understand your goals, start your treatment and plan your recovery.
#### Follow-Up Consultation (30 minutes)
The physio checks on how you have been, progresses your treatment and moves you closer to your goal.
#### Extended Consultation (40 minutes)
For patients who have more than one problem and need more time.

## ONLINE VIDEO CONSULTATIONS
Most of the services above are also available online via video conferencing. Video is handy when you need to quickly talk about a new injury and find out how to tackle it, or when you are further along in recovery and the rehabilitation program is being adjusted. You can also speak to the clinic online via video link to find out whether and how they can help.
### Video Programs
There are 4 programs to choose from.
#### Pay-As-You-Go
One video consultation (either 30 minutes or 40 minutes), a personalised rehabilitation program, and unlimited access to physio support via App messaging for 1 week.
#### 4-Week Program
One 30 minute video consultation, a personalised and adaptive rehabilitation program, unlimited access to physio support via App messaging for 4 weeks, and a 10 minute video review consultation at 4 weeks.
#### 8-Week Program
Two 30 minute video consultations, unlimited access to physio support via App messaging for 8 weeks, and a 10 minute video review consultation at 8 weeks.
#### 12-Week Program
Three 30 minute video consultations, unlimited access to physio support via App messaging for 12 weeks, and a 10 minute video review consultation at 12 weeks.
