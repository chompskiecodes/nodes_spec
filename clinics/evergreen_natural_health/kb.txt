Your practitioner will begin by checking your radial pulse and may also look at your tongue and other observational signs used in Chinese medicine. ## Acupuncture
### About Acupuncture
Acupuncture is the insertion of very thin needles into specific areas of the body to trigger the body's natural healing mechanisms. It stimulates blood flow to the area with the problem so the body can better repair it. It is commonly used to support the management of pain and other symptoms. It can be combined with cupping therapy if required. No home exercises are required. Minor pain at the insertion site or minor bruising may occur at times in some patients.
### Muscle Reset Acupuncture
Evergreen is the home of Muscle Reset Acupuncture. The practitioner tests your muscles to find the specific muscles that are not working well. Acupuncture with gentle TENS stimulation (the gentle current feels like light tapping and often moves the muscle a little) is then used to reset those muscles. Treatment often finishes with guasha or cupping to clear the muscles and improve blood flow to the area. The muscle strength is usually tested, then the acupuncture is done, then the muscle is retested. There are no exercises to do; you relax while you receive the acupuncture.
#### Who Muscle Reset Acupuncture Is For
Anyone with pain in almost any part of the body, anyone who has not gained results from other forms of treatment, anyone with pain that keeps returning, and anyone with pain, weakness or restriction preventing them from competing in their sport.
#### Common Symptoms Treated
Back pain, headaches, sciatica, jaw pain, neck pain, and tight or achy muscles.
#### Treatment Frequency
When you book you are assessed by a Registered Acupuncturist and then receive Muscle Reset Acupuncture. One treatment a week for three weeks is usually recommended, followed by a reassessment.
### Body Symmetry Testing
Body Symmetry Testing is a short series of muscle tests that shows which muscles are working well and which are not, to find the root cause of pain. It is available free at the Bairnsdale clinic, takes about 10 minutes, and is done by Dr Bruce, who will tell you what he finds and how he can help. You then either book in for treatment (acupuncture and/or massage to treat the cause) or take away what you have learned. The free test is subject to the free consultation terms above. These are genuine free appointments not subsidised by any other service.

## Medicinal Herbs
Herbal formulae are mixed in ready-to-take liquid form and taken daily. Bruce will diagnose the cause of your health issue and then prescribe herbs designed to reduce or eliminate the symptoms. Herbs are often combined with acupuncture for the best result.
### Herbs and Medications
Medicinal herbs can be taken at the same time as medications. Bruce will review your medications to ensure there are no contraindications with the herbs prescribed. Always consult your GP or specialist before discontinuing any medication.
### Side Effects
Side effects from the herbal formulae are rare. Unwanted effects are usually digestive, for example stomach upset or altered bowel habits. If any occur, the herbalist will change the formula. With acupuncture, minor pain at the insertion site or minor bruising may occur at times in some patients.

## Cold and Flu Herbs
### Service Overview
A tailored 5 day course of high-strength liquid medicinal herbs for cold and flu symptoms, with no consultation fees and no waiting times. Herbs are delivered by Express Post Australia-wide. A formula of up to 7 herbs is mixed for you based on a Medical Questionnaire you complete when you order. The herbs are in liquid format in an ethanol base. Pour some into the supplied measuring cup, add a little water and drink it, two or three times a day depending on your prescription. The herbs have a strong flavour; a sweetener can be added or they can be taken with juice.

A 5 day course of herbs is $85. Express Post is an additional $20. To order, click the Order Herbs button on the website and fill in the health questionnaire.
### Cold and Flu Herbs FAQ
#### What do the herbs do?
The herbs are anti-viral, anti-bacterial, immune-boosting, chest-clearing, nose clearing and energy-giving. A formula is made up specifically for you to help you get over your cold or flu faster.
#### Can I take your herbs if I am already taking other medications?
Yes, the herbs prescribed are ok to take while on other medications.
#### Will one 5 day course be enough?
For most people, yes. Sometimes for strong colds or flus you may need to take another course of herbs.
#### Are there any side-effects from taking the herbs?
Side-effects are rare, but the most common side-effect of herbs is to loosen bowel motions. This is asked about in the Medical Questionnaire and the herbs are adjusted accordingly.
#### Do you charge a consultation fee?
No, there are no consultation fees.
#### Do the herbs work if you have Covid?
Covid is a virus, as is the common cold and also flu. Antiviral herbs are used and your immune system is boosted. Antibiotics do not treat viruses, but specific herbs do.
#### How long do the herbs last?
You typically get a 5 day course of herbs. The herbs will last at least 6 months in the bottle.
#### Can children take the herbs?
Yes, but the dosage is adjusted for each child based on the medical questionnaire you complete when you order.
#### Do you provide support after I order?
If you have any questions, you can access support after you order.
#### Do you keep a record?
Yes, a patient record is created for you. Patient records are kept electronically, are secure and are governed by the clinic's Privacy Policy.
#### Can I order your herbs to have on me in case I get sick?
Yes. The herbs you receive will last up to a year if stored in a cool dark place. When you fill in your questionnaire, complete the symptoms you normally get with cold and flu. This can also be a good option if you are going to travel.
