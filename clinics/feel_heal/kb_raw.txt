Chronic Pain Management

Dry Needling

This technique involves the insertion of thin needles into trigger points, releasing tension and promoting healing. Our experienced practitioners use dry needling as a powerful tool to address muscle pain, improve flexibility, and enhance overall mobility.

Cupping

A traditional therapy that has stood the test of time, cupping involves the use of suction cups to improve blood flow, reduce muscle tension, and promote healing. Our cupping services are tailored to complement your overall wellness plan.
