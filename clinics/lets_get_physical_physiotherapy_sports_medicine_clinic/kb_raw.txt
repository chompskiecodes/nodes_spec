Dry Needling

Dry needling is a therapeutic technique that targets trigger points in muscles, helping to relieve pain and improve mobility. It promotes blood flow to the injured area facilitating healing and reducing muscle tension. Many patients experience a significant decrease in pain and an increase in range of motion following treatment. Overall, dry needling can an effective part of a comprehensive rehabilitation plan various injuries.

Manual Therapy

Manual therapy in physiotherapy offers numerous benefits that enhance recovery and improve overall well-being. It helps reduce pain and muscle tension increases flexibility, and promotes better circulation. Additionally manual therapy, including sports massage, can improve joint mobility and function, allowing for a more effective rehabilitation process. This targeted approach supports athletes in maintaining peak performance and preventing.

Exercise Rehabiliation

Exercise provides essential benefits that promote healing, including improving performance and reducing pain. By enhancing blood circulation, it accelerates recovery and strengthens muscles and for better overall performance. Additionally, exercise releases endorphins, which act as natural painkillers, making the healing process more comfortable. Pilates is an effective method for enhancing strength and rehabilitating core, promoting stability and flexibility while helping to prevent injuries.

What conditions do we treat?

We treat all types of musculoskeletal injuries from everyday aches and pains to complex injuries. We look after any type of muscle, joint  or nerve pain or injury. Our goal is to not only ease your pain, but to find out why the injury occurred in the first place and ensure the injury doesn't occur again.

Examples of injuries we treat include back and neck pain, shoulder elbow and wrist, hip knee and ankle, sporting injuries, workplace and motor vehicle injuries, post operative injuries, headaches and TMJ, chronic pain, balance, mobility and falls prevention to name but a few.

Do I need a referral for Physio?

No you do not need a referral from a GP to attend physio. However you can get an EPC plan from your GP which entitles you to 5 subsidised physio visits per calendar year from medicare.

Do we accept private health insurance?

Yes we accept all health insurance providers on the day via HICAPS.

Can we refer for Imaging such as X-Ray or MRI?
