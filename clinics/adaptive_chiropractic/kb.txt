## Chiropractic Care
### What Chiropractic Care Addresses
The nervous system, protected by the spine, controls every cell, tissue, and organ, coordinating movement and the body's response to stress. Misalignments in the spine, known as subluxations, can disrupt nervous system function and affect health and wellbeing. Chiropractic care aims to keep the spine aligned so the nervous system functions optimally, relieving pain and supporting the body's natural healing process.

### Conditions Chiropractic Care Can Help With
Back and neck pain, jaw pain and TMJ, headaches and migraines, pregnancy/pelvic discomfort, poor posture and rounded shoulders, nerve pain and sciatica, vertigo and dizziness, disc injuries, muscular tension.

### Chiropractic Care May Also Improve
Flexibility, energy levels, immune system function, coordination and mobility, stress management, productivity and concentration, emotional wellbeing, sporting performance, sleep and relaxation.

### Who The Clinic Helps
Adaptive Chiropractic treats people from all over Melbourne, particularly Richmond, Abbotsford, Burnley and Cremorne — from athletes and gym-goers to office professionals and tradies, and anyone wanting to feel better.

### What To Expect
The initial chiropractic consultation runs around 45 minutes and includes a detailed examination (orthopedic, neurological and postural screening) plus the clinic's on-site scanning technology. X-rays are considered case-by-case, not routine. If chiropractic care is suitable, the chiropractor provides a detailed report and treatment goal; if not, the patient is referred to a more suitable health professional. The first treatment (up to 30 minutes) can usually happen straight after the initial exam, and most patients notice results from the very first treatment. Regular follow-up adjustments (around 10-20 minutes each) help maintain alignment and progress toward the treatment plan's goals.

### Scanning Technology
Every new patient receives three non-invasive scans as part of their initial consultation (around 15 minutes total, results explained the same day), using Insight Scanning Technology:
- **Neuro thermal scan** — infrared thermal readings along the paraspinal muscles; temperature changes along the spine can indicate how well nerves are supplying organs, glands and vessels.
- **Static EMG scan** — surface electromyography (sEMG) sensors along the spine measure nerve signals to muscles, detecting over- or under-active (hypertonic/hypotonic) muscle activity that affects posture and alignment.
- **Pulse Wave Profiler (PWP)** — measures Heart Rate Variability (HRV) to assess how the nervous system is coping with stress, and tracks recovery over the course of care.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What types of problems does chiropractic help?**
Chiropractic is best known for helping with low back pain, neck pain and headaches, but chiropractors also achieve success with a huge range of other problems because chiropractic addresses the health of the whole person — keeping the nervous system free from interference supports better overall health.

**Do I need a referral from a GP?**
No referral is needed for a chiropractic visit. Chiropractors are licensed primary health care professionals who examine, diagnose and care for patients directly.

**Will my private health insurance cover chiropractic care?**
Most health plans cover chiropractic up to a certain annual maximum. The clinic is a registered HICAPS provider, so eligible patients can claim on the spot.

**How many chiropractic adjustments will I need?**
This varies per patient and their individual health goals. Many patients notice progress within a week or two of frequent visits, and visits become less frequent as the spine stabilises.

**How long before I feel better?**
This varies from person to person depending on factors like how long the problem has existed, appointment attendance, rest, exercise, nutrition and smoking. Most patients notice progress within a short period of following the chiropractor's recommendations.

**How long will I need chiropractic care?**
This is the patient's own decision. Many patients choose to continue with scheduled wellness care after their initial period of care to maintain progress and catch new problems early.

**Is there a chiropractor near me in Richmond?**
Yes — 276 Lennox Street, Richmond, just off Bridge Road, easily reached by public transport (West Richmond Station on the Hurstbridge or Mernda line; Bridge Road trams 48 & 75).
