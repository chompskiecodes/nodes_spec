## Physiotherapy
### Our Approach
Physio treatments aim to restore and facilitate movement, using progressive treatment techniques for the full spectrum of injuries. The approach is hands on, in private consulting rooms, with a strong emphasis on education and exercise as a pathway to recovery. The therapists take the time to teach you about your injury and equip you with strategies to solve it. Every injury will benefit from movement and exercise in some form.
### Common Issues Treated
Our Physiotherapists offer a wide variety of treatments to deliver an individualised management plan, working as part of a multi-disciplinary team. Whether an acute injury, a long-standing problem or pre/post-operative, they work together to find the best treatment for you. Common issues treated include sports injuries, neck and back pain, headaches, management of chronic conditions, pre and post-operative management, injury prevention, sprains and strains, postural and biomechanical issues, fall prevention, general mobility, Worker's Compensation and Motor Vehicle Claims, and education and rehabilitation.
### Treatments
#### Manual Therapy
Therapists are trained in hands-on techniques for diagnosis and treatment including soft tissue treatment (massage, trigger point release, myofascial release, deep tissue massage), joint mobilisation and manipulations, neuromuscular techniques, and facilitation of posture, motor control and muscle retraining.
#### Taping
A range of taping techniques are used as an adjunct to physiotherapy treatment to provide support and stability to the injured area, relieve pain by reducing load on the painful area, re-train posture and encourage normal movement, and aid confidence when returning to sport.
#### Dry Needling
Dry Needling is a muscle releasing technique that works to resolve painful and taut bands within muscle fibres (trigger points), often referred to as muscle knots. Our Physiotherapists are trained in Dry Needling and use it in conjunction with other techniques.
#### Electrotherapeutic Modalities
As part of injury management and rehabilitation, modalities such as Ultrasound, Interferential Therapy and Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation (TENS) are used for pain relief, managing inflammation and aiding the body's natural healing processes.
#### Vertigo
BPPV or Positional Vertigo is a common condition that our Physiotherapists are experienced in treating. Vertigo can cause episodes of dizziness triggered by movements of your head. There are manoeuvres that can resolve the dizziness.
#### Education
Through education the clinic aims to ensure you leave confident about the cause of your symptoms, how to manage them and how to maximise your health.
#### Hydrotherapy
Hydrotherapy is water-based rehabilitation or aquatic exercise in a temperature-controlled environment, offered to all patients at Ellenbrook Physiotherapy as part of their rehabilitation. Exercises include walking and functional movements such as squatting and lunging. You do not need any experience in swimming as the pool is at a depth suitable for almost all heights. Sessions are run by one of the experienced physiotherapists who individually tailors an exercise program based on your needs.
#### Who hydrotherapy suits
Hydrotherapy can help a wide range of acute and musculoskeletal, cardiovascular and neurological conditions, including osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, fibromyalgia, common musculoskeletal pain such as low back pain, post-surgical cases such as joint replacements, arthroscopies and joint reconstructions, cerebral palsy and multiple sclerosis.
#### When hydrotherapy is not recommended
Hydrotherapy is not recommended if you have or have had a heart attack or unstable heart conditions, skin or wound infections, uncontrolled epilepsy, uncontrolled diabetes, incontinence, fever or recent neurological events.
