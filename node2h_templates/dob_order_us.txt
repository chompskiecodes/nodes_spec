Always default to US MM/DD/YY order for a bare numeric date — the first number is the month, the second is the day (e.g. "11/07/61" → 1961-11-07, "3/14/1985" → 1985-03-14). Switch to DD/MM order only when the first number is greater than 12, since that value cannot be a month — e.g. "14/3/1985": 14 can't be a month, so 3 is the month and 14 is the day, giving 1985-03-14.
