# Strides Health Group

## About Strides Health Group
### Overview
Physiotherapy for children, adults and athletes at Strides Health Group in Narre Warren. Selected appointments are also available in Nunawading. The clinic offers four services: Little Strides (paediatric physiotherapy), Adult Physiotherapy, Strides Performance (sports physiotherapy for young athletes) and Neurological and Disability Physiotherapy. You do not need to know which treatment you require before booking. The team will help identify the most appropriate starting point.
### What To Expect At A First Appointment
The physiotherapist starts with your story, understanding what has been happening, what you are concerned about and what you are hoping to achieve. They complete a thorough assessment, explain what they have found, answer your questions and make sure you understand your options. Together you develop a plan that reflects your goals, your lifestyle and your stage of life. You leave with a clearer understanding of what is happening, practical next steps and a plan. The plan is adapted as you progress.

## Little Strides Paediatric Physiotherapy
### About Little Strides
Paediatric physiotherapy for babies, children and adolescents in Narre Warren, with selected appointments in Nunawading. It helps families understand their child's development, answer questions about movement, pain and participation, and access practical guidance. Sometimes that means therapy and sometimes reassurance. Parents do not need to be certain there is a problem before asking.
### Common Reasons Parents Book
#### Is my child developing as expected
Parents wondering whether their child is meeting movement milestones.
#### Tripping or falling
A child who keeps tripping or falling.
#### New diagnosis
A child who has just received a diagnosis.
#### Pain
A child who has pain.
#### Reassurance
Parents who want someone to check that everything is okay.
### Conditions Mentioned In Paediatric Care
#### Toe walking
A paediatric physiotherapy assessment can check how a child moves, whether the heels can come down comfortably and whether another health professional should be involved.
#### Torticollis in babies
Babies who consistently tilt the head to one side, turn more easily in one direction or feed more comfortably on one side. A paediatric physiotherapist looks at the baby's whole movement pattern and offers practical positioning ideas for feeding, carrying, floor play and daily routines, and liaises with the GP or other health professionals when needed.
#### Plagiocephaly (positional head flattening) in babies
Physiotherapy can help with positioning, play and monitoring of head shape and developmental movement, with referral to a specialist when needed.
#### Developmental delay, coordination and gross motor skills
Physiotherapy can support movement, confidence and everyday participation.
#### Children with autism
Physiotherapy can support movement, play and participation, with goals chosen with the child and family.
### Working With The Wider Team
The team works alongside paediatricians, GPs, maternal and child health nurses, occupational therapists, speech pathologists, teachers, educators, orthotists and other health professionals.

## Adult Physiotherapy
### About Adult Physiotherapy
Individualised physiotherapy for pain, injury, dizziness, balance and mobility at the Narre Warren clinic, with selected appointments in Nunawading. You do not need to know what is wrong before you book. If physiotherapy is the right approach the team will explain why, and if another pathway is more appropriate they will help guide you in the right direction.
### Conditions Treated
Back and neck pain. Shoulder, hip and knee pain. Tendon and sporting injuries. Arthritis and joint pain. Headaches. Vertigo and dizziness. Balance problems and falls. Post-operative rehabilitation. Persistent pain. Respiratory conditions affecting movement.
### Treatment May Include
Hands-on physiotherapy. Exercise rehabilitation. Strength and conditioning. Acupuncture and dry needling (where appropriate). Vestibular rehabilitation. Balance training. Education and self-management. Return to work, sport or everyday activities.
### Dizziness And Balance
The clinic provides individual physiotherapy assessment and care for dizziness and balance concerns. The first appointment focuses on understanding your symptoms, checking whether physiotherapy is appropriate and agreeing on a safe plan. You may be referred to your GP or another health professional when needed. Adult physiotherapy can assess balance, walking, strength, dizziness and everyday mobility.
### Knee Osteoarthritis And GLA:D
Daniel has completed GLA:D Australia clinician training. Strides Health Group does not currently offer the full formal GLA:D programme. Where appropriate, relevant education and exercise principles may be incorporated into individual physiotherapy sessions and tailored to your goals.

## Strides Performance Sports Physiotherapy
### About Strides Performance
Sports physiotherapy in Narre Warren for swimmers and young athletes through injury, rehabilitation and return to sport, with selected appointments in Nunawading. Care considers the athlete's symptoms, sport, training load, stage of development and goals.
### Who It Helps
Athletes recovering from injury, returning to training or competition, experiencing growth-related pain, preparing for an important season, looking to reduce their risk of injury, wanting to improve strength, movement and resilience, or trying to understand why injuries keep returning.
### Conditions Supported
Tendon injuries. Growth-related conditions. Joint and muscle injuries. Concussion rehabilitation. Running injuries. Swimming-related shoulder pain. Load management. Return-to-sport planning. Performance support.
### Athlete Screening
Athlete screening is tailored to the person and the sport. It can combine a physiotherapy history and examination with selected physical tests. Where indicated, VALD ForceDecks and Dynamo+ are used to assess selected jump performance, force production and strength measures. Results are translated into clear priorities for training, rehabilitation or referral. Screening is not a requirement to participate in sport. To discuss an appointment, contact Strides Health Group on 0411 876 851 or admin@strideshealth.com.au.
### Working With Parents And Coaches
With the athlete's permission, the team works alongside parents, coaches, strength and conditioning staff, teachers, sports physicians and other health professionals.

## Neurological And Disability Physiotherapy
### About Neurological And Disability Physiotherapy
Individualised rehabilitation for children and adults to improve movement, participation, confidence and independence, at the Narre Warren clinic with selected appointments in Nunawading. Call 0411 876 851 to make an enquiry. Contact the clinic to discuss needs and arrange an appointment before booking; NDIS participants should contact the clinic to discuss service suitability before booking.
### Conditions
Cerebral palsy. Stroke. Parkinson's disease. Multiple sclerosis. Acquired brain injury. Traumatic brain injury. Functional neurological disorder. Peripheral neurological conditions. Balance disorders. Other neurological conditions affecting movement and participation.
### Rehabilitation Plan May Include
Functional movement training. Balance and falls prevention. Walking and gait retraining. Equipment and mobility advice. Education for you, your family and carers. Home exercise programs. Collaborative care with your broader healthcare team.
### Working With Your Care Team
Where appropriate the team works closely with families, carers, support coordinators, occupational therapists, speech pathologists, exercise physiologists, medical specialists and other members of your healthcare team. With your consent, relevant findings can be shared. Family members and support workers can help carry agreed strategies into everyday routines.
### Accessing Services
Physiotherapy can be accessed privately or through Medicare (where eligible), DVA, TAC, WorkCover or the NDIS (self-managed or plan-managed).
