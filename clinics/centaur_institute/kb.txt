# Centaur Institute

## About Centaur Institute
### Overview
Centaur Institute (formerly OSVi, Orthosport Victoria Institute) is a musculoskeletal healthcare centre based on Bridge Road in Richmond, Melbourne. Patients are cared for by a combined team of orthopaedic surgeons, physiotherapists, sports physicians and doctors, exercise physiologists, strength and conditioning coaches, podiatrists, clinical pilates instructors and clinical researchers. The team manages all patients regardless of age, ability or activity profile. Centaur Institute works as a collaborative vertical model of care, taking patients from initial presentation through diagnosis, non-surgical or surgical treatment and rehabilitation.
### Facility
The purpose-built facility at 132A Bridge Road, Richmond has clinical consulting and treatment rooms, a strength and conditioning gymnasium, and a biomechanical laboratory and testing facility. The Research Department works closely with the clinical team and tracks patient progress for both surgical and non-surgical patients.

## Sports Injury Emergency Clinic
### About the Emergency Clinic
The Centaur Institute Sports Injury Emergency Clinic in Richmond treats non-life-threatening emergency sports injuries that can be treated by a doctor or physiotherapist, so patients avoid waiting in a hospital emergency department. It receives appropriate patients as walk-ins and can provide patient transportation services.
### Patient Process
On arrival the team guides you through your current condition and assists with patient details, then escorts you to a specialist sports clinician for expedited assessment and treatment. The clinician determines the treatment plan. Where further rehabilitation is required, patients can transition to ongoing physiotherapy and rehabilitation at Centaur Institute. Where indicated, the team can also arrange a consultation with an orthopaedic surgeon.
### Opening Hours

### What the Emergency Clinic Sees
Any acute sporting injury (occurred in the last 6 weeks). All soft tissue injuries including severe ankle sprains and serious knee and shoulder injuries. Broken bones not obviously requiring urgent surgery or affecting the pelvis and spine. Open wounds, abrasions and haematomas. Facial injuries including eye and nose injuries; oral injuries can be treated, though some dental injuries may be referred to acute dental care. Dislocations of the fingers, toes, knee cap and shoulder. Minor head injuries and concussion.
### What the Emergency Clinic Does Not See
Any chronic sports injury (older than 6 weeks). Non-sporting injuries such as falls and car accidents. More serious injuries requiring higher acuity treatment. Very unwell appearing or drowsy patients. Severe pain likely to require pain medication through a drip. Broken bones with severe deformity or puncture of the skin that may require more urgent surgery. Possible spinal injuries. Possible pelvic fractures. Possible internal bleeding or collapsed lung. Severe pain that does not respond to pain-relieving medications given through injection or nasal spray.

## Orthopaedic Surgery
### About Orthopaedic Surgery
Centaur Institute orthopaedic surgeons manage sports injuries, traumatic injuries and degenerative disorders of the musculoskeletal system. Depending on the injury, a patient may be referred to sports medicine physicians, physiotherapists or exercise physiologists for conservative management before deciding whether to proceed with surgery. After surgery, post-operative physiotherapy at Centaur Institute supports recovery, so care is coordinated through the acute and rehabilitation phases.
### Surgical Areas
#### Knee Surgery
Total knee replacement, partial knee replacement, patella stabilisation, ACL reconstruction, knee arthroscopy, osteotomy for knee arthritis, and computer-assisted surgery and robotics.
#### Hip Surgery
Total hip replacement, revision of total hip replacement, hip arthroscopy and management of hip arthritis.
#### Foot and Ankle Surgery
Fractures, sprains, bunions and chronic instability, with surgical and non-surgical care.
#### Shoulder Surgery
Shoulder arthroscopy, shoulder replacement and rotator cuff repair, including for dislocation and arthritis.
#### Fractures and Trauma Surgery
Fractures and tendon ruptures.
#### Elbow Surgery
Elbow arthroscopy and elbow replacement.
#### Hand and Wrist Surgery
Thumb and finger joint arthroscopy, joint replacement and release of Dupuytren's contracture.
### Hip Surgery Detail
Care covers the full spectrum of hip conditions, from sports-related impingement in young athletes to advanced arthritis requiring joint replacement. Hip arthroscopy and hip preservation can reshape the joint and repair damaged tissue for hip impingement (FAI) or labral tears in younger and active patients. Revision hip replacement replaces or repairs a previous hip replacement that has become loose, painful or worn. Rehabilitation is delivered onsite by the physiotherapy and strength and conditioning team, with movement analysis through the 3D Biomechanics and Performance Lab where appropriate.
#### Hip Conditions Treated
Hip osteoarthritis, hip impingement (femoroacetabular impingement), labral tears, hip fractures, avascular necrosis, gluteal tendinopathy and trochanteric pain, and failed or painful previous hip replacement.

The sports podiatry clinic assesses and manages all foot and ankle injuries. ## 3D Biomechanics and Performance Lab
### About the Lab
The Biomechanics and Performance Testing team of sports physiotherapy practitioners, biomechanics specialists and researchers offers advanced movement and performance assessment to athletes and patients undergoing rehabilitation or looking to improve performance. Biomechanical analysis uses a 25 metre runway embedded with two Bertec force plates and an Xsens 3D motion capture system. Strength and performance testing uses a Humac isokinetic dynamometer, VALD ForceFrame, VALD ForceDecks, VALD SmartSpeed Timing Gates, VALD Dynamo and VALD NordBord. Ligament laxity assessment and patient-reported outcome measures (PROMS) are also applied.
### Reporting and Results
Results come with immediate biofeedback and same-day reporting. Reporting options let patients take the report back to their treating clinician or receive in-house support. The data creates a movement and performance profile that can become part of a rehabilitation plan with the physiotherapy team, by benchmarking against normative data, identifying detrimental biomechanical patterns, addressing residual physical deficits and developing targeted interventions.
### Streams
The lab streams are ACLR, lower limb rehab, Fit For Life assessment and athletic performance.

## Strength and Conditioning
### About Strength and Conditioning
Strength and conditioning coaches apply sports science to improve speed, endurance, agility, power, stability, strength and mobility, for athletes at all levels from aspiring juniors to elite competitors. The Richmond strength and conditioning gym integrates biomechanical analysis to assess movement patterns and create customised sports-specific programs. Physiotherapists and nutritionists are available to program participants at a discounted rate.
### Free Performance Assessment
A free performance assessment with a professional S&C coach includes a jump test using force plates, a speed test with timing gates, and a strength test using Nordbord hamstring analysis. Athletes are offered a free performance assessment to come and see the gym.
### Strength and Conditioning Programs
#### Core Development Program
For athletes aged 13 and over. A 12 week strength and conditioning program with a core performance assessment, a dedicated coach, an individual program with Team Builder software access, discounted allied health services, discounted nutrition services and no minimum term. It is offered as 2 gym sessions per week or 3 gym sessions per week, priced per week; the prices are not shown in the website text. The program focuses on strength, rate of force development, upper and lower body power and injury prevention. It begins with a baseline assessment using VALD sport science equipment covering acceleration speed, vertical jump, reactive strength, hip adduction and abduction strength and eccentric hamstring strength. For enquiries, fill out the Athlete Enquiry Form and staff will be in touch.
#### Rehab Performance Program
For athletes of all ages returning from injury. Priced at 129 per week for a minimum of 12 weeks. Inclusions are a performance assessment, a biomechanic assessment, an athlete biomechanic and performance report, a dedicated strength and conditioning coach, unlimited gym sessions per week, an individual program designed from the biomechanic and performance report with Team Builder software access, and discounted allied health services. The biomechanics assessment uses motion capture to analyse running mechanics, change of direction and jumping technique. Unlimited access to the gym and on-field running sessions, supervised by coaches, with controlled progression of load and movement complexity. Athletes can access Centaur Institute's physiotherapy service where clinical assessment or treatment is needed. For enquiries, fill out the Athlete Enquiry Form.
#### Fit For Life Program
For individuals of all ages, focused on movement competency and technique. A 12 week strength and conditioning program with discounted nutrition services. It begins with an initial performance assessment using VALD sport science equipment, then gym access twice a week with coaches guiding each session. Physiotherapists and nutritionists are available at a discounted rate. For enquiries, fill out the Fit For Life Enquiry Form.
