# Morgana Walker Psychology

## About

### Practice Overview
Morgana Walker Psychology provides client-centred, trauma-informed, evidence-based psychological support for neurodivergent individuals, families, and professionals across the lifespan. Morgana Walker Psychology was previously known as Bee Kind Psychology.

### Practice Approach
The practice is built on a neuro-affirming, strengths-based approach: neurodiversity is treated as a natural and valuable part of human experience rather than a deficit to be fixed, and clients are supported to understand how their mind works so they can build on their strengths and navigate challenges with more confidence.

Support draws on a deep understanding of ADHD and neurodiversity, providing clear, evidence-informed guidance that translates into everyday life rather than just theory, whether a client is exploring ADHD, supporting a neurodivergent child, or making sense of their own experience.

Sessions are warm, respectful, and collaborative, with space for clients to be themselves at their own pace. Care is practical: clients leave sessions with strategies they can use right away, a clearer understanding of what is happening for them or their child, and support that fits their real-world context.

Care is whole-person and whole-family focused. Whether working with children, adults, or families, support is designed around the whole system, including parents, relationships, learning environments, and daily life. Sessions also provide space to ask questions, explore without pressure, and make sense of things at each client's own pace, without judgement.

### How We Work
The practice combines evidence-based care with a deep respect for each person's story, guided by three practice principles. Neurodiversity-Affirming: the full spectrum of minds, including autistic, ADHD, dyslexic and beyond, is celebrated, and services are designed to honour identity and empower growth. Trauma-Informed: safety and trust are at the heart of the work, recognising the impact of trauma and creating space for healing to begin, free from judgment or pressure. Client-Centred Practice: care is developed collaboratively to align with each person's values, strengths, and priorities, using structured methods supported by current research and clinical guidelines.

The practice is also guided by four values: respect for every journey, collaboration and empowerment, transparency and choice, and continuous learning.

### Registered Psychologist
Morgana Walker is a registered psychologist specialising in neuro-affirming assessment and therapy. She blends evidence-based practice with a gentle, practical approach that centres each person's strengths and sensory needs. She works with children, adolescents, adults, and families, supporting skill development, emotional regulation, and wellbeing through a practical, strengths-based approach.

Morgana is a Registered Psychologist and a member of the Australian Psychological Society (MAPS). She is a neurodiversity-affirming practitioner, keynote conference presenter, award-winning educator, VIT registered teacher with special education qualifications, and a clinically-trained play therapist. Her specialisation is neuro-affirming assessment and therapy across all ages.

### Areas of Practice
Areas of practice include autism, ADHD, trauma, play therapy, child development, parenting skills, and school-based issues.

### Who the Practice Supports
The practice supports children and young people, adults, families and parents, educators and schools, allied health professionals, and the global community, across the whole lifespan.

## Services and Fees

### Therapy for Children and Teens
Morgana Walker offers personalised counselling, assessments, and therapy for children and families. The practice works closely with parents to support emotional wellbeing and development in a warm, child-friendly space. Services include support for emotional and behavioural concerns, psychological assessments, parent coaching and family therapy, school and kinder visits, and collaborative care with other professionals.

### Individual Therapy
Individual therapy supports adults facing stress, relationship challenges, parenting, perinatal mental health, ADHD, or uncertainty about the future. Sessions take a warm, down-to-earth approach focused on helping each person feel safe, understood, and empowered, working together to find the path that best suits their needs.

### Assessments (Cognitive and Behavioural)
Tailored assessments are available for children and adults to support learning, behaviour, and emotional wellbeing. Referrals can be made by a GP, paediatrician, school, or directly through the website, so no referral is required. Each assessment includes a detailed report and a feedback session with practical recommendations; for children, feedback can be shared with schools to support collaborative care. Assessment types offered include educational assessments, behavioural assessments, and adult assessments. Medicare rebates are not available for assessment services.

#### General Adult / Child Behavioural Assessment
Two 60 minute in-person sessions, report preparation, and a 30 minute online feedback session. $625.

#### Formal Diagnostic Adult Behavioural Assessment
In-person appointments, report preparation, and a feedback session included. $1,500.

#### Child / Adolescent Cognitive Assessment
One 60 minute in-person session, report preparation, and a 30 minute online feedback session.

#### Child / Adolescent Diagnostic Behavioural Assessment
One 120 minute in-person session, report preparation, and a 30 minute parent feedback session.

#### Child / Adolescent Psycho-Educational Assessment
WISC and WIAT testing across two 60 minute in-person sessions, report preparation, and a 30 minute parent feedback session. $3,000.

### Play Therapy
Play therapy supports children and families through challenges such as anxiety, mood changes, behavioural concerns, sleep or eating difficulties, emotional regulation, trauma, and school-related issues. Sessions use play-based, strengths-focused approaches designed to help children feel safe and understood, with a collaborative style that empowers families to work toward meaningful goals together.

### Family and Parent Support
Family and parent support offers guidance to help parents develop effective strategies and strengthen family relationships, focusing on positive parenting, communication, and behavioural management.

#### Parent Check-In Sessions
Parent Check-In sessions are available at any time throughout a child's treatment. These sessions offer an opportunity to discuss the child's progress, therapeutic goals, and any concerns, helping ensure care remains collaborative and responsive to the family's needs. Appointments can be scheduled online or in person.

### School Observations
School observations are conducted in educational settings to understand student behaviour, learning styles, and classroom interactions, with insights used to inform strategies for improved learning outcomes.

### Non-Directive Pregnancy Counselling
Non-directive pregnancy counselling offers gentle, Medicare-supported counselling (Item 81000) for anyone feeling overwhelmed, uncertain, or in need of support during or after a pregnancy. Up to three confidential sessions are available to explore emotions, options, and support needs, whether navigating grief, big decisions, or early parenting.

### Multidisciplinary Case Conferencing
Multidisciplinary case conferencing involves collaborative sessions with multiple professionals to coordinate care and support, ensuring a supportive and inclusive approach to each client's needs.

### Workshops and Public Speaking
Workshops and public speaking sessions are professional presentations and interactive learning experiences aimed at education and skill development, designed for schools, organisations, and community groups. Morgana is also available for keynote presentations, professional conferences, parent education events, leadership forums, and corporate wellbeing events, covering topics such as ADHD and autism across the lifespan, neurodivergent wellbeing and burnout prevention, masking and identity, supporting neurodivergent girls and women, and practical strategies for parenting neurodivergent children.

### Speaking Engagements
Morgana delivers keynote presentations, professional training, and conference sessions for professionals, parents, educators, allied health clinicians, organisations, and community leaders, combining clinical expertise, lived experience, and practical strategies. Featured speaking engagements include the Child & Adolescent Mental Health Conference on vocational vulnerability and advocacy in youth mental health, the Australasian ADHD Professionals Association (AADPA) Conference on masking and systemic practice change, the Her Mind Her Health Conference on neurodivergent experiences across the lifespan with a focus on women and girls, and the Neurodivergence Wellbeing Conference on practical tools for parenting neurodivergent children.

### Fees
All fees are in Australian dollars (AUD). Medicare, Private Health Insurance, and NDIS (self-managed) are accepted for eligible therapy services. Medicare rebates are not available for assessment services.

#### Child Therapy
Initial consultation is a 50 minute session at the psychologist fee of $330. Subsequent consultations are $300. Weekday group therapy for children aged 6 to 12, with a maximum of 6 participants, is $150.

#### Adult Therapy
Adult individual sessions accept Medicare, Private Health, and NDIS self-managed funding. Session types include a standard 50 minute consultation, ADHD coaching, a parent check-in, and a 30 minute online consultation.

### Cancellation Policy
If an appointment needs to be cancelled or rescheduled, at least 48 hours' notice is required to avoid a cancellation fee, as this time is specially set aside for the client. Less than 48 hours' notice incurs a cancellation fee of 50% of the service cost. Less than 24 hours' notice incurs a cancellation fee of 100% of the service cost, which must be paid before further appointments can be made.

### Downloadable Resources
A downloadable resource, the Neurodivergent Parent Burnout Toolkit, is available as an instant-download PDF with fillable worksheets. It is a practical, evidence-informed workbook for neurodivergent families to identify burnout risk, map sensory stressors, plan recovery, and build sustainable routines, aimed at parents, carers, and allied professionals.

## Neurodivergent Parent Academy

### About the Academy
The Neurodivergent Parent Academy provides practical support, evidence-informed education, and real-life strategies for parents, carers, educators, and professionals supporting neurodivergent individuals. It is open to families, educators, and professionals worldwide, with no prior psychology knowledge required. Learning formats include live online masterclasses, on-demand recordings and replays, intensive workshops, and professional development training for schools and organisations. Certificates of completion are available for most programs, and many programs are relevant to NDIS participants for family and carer capacity building through parent and carer training pathways.

### Course Catalogue
The course catalogue covers the full neurodevelopmental lifespan across six series. The Foundations Series includes Understanding Autism, Understanding ADHD, and Understanding Neurodiversity. The Emotional Wellbeing Series includes Emotional Regulation, Understanding Anxiety, and Managing Family Stress. The Behaviour and Support Series includes Behaviour Through a Neurodiversity Lens and Meltdowns and Shutdowns. The Executive Functioning Series includes Supporting Executive Function and Building Independence. The School and Education Series includes School Success and School Refusal and School Distress. The Adolescent and Adult Series includes Preparing for Adulthood and Neurodiversity in Young Adulthood. Individual masterclasses start from $99 AUD.

### Who the Academy Is For
The Academy is open to parents and carers supporting autism, ADHD, PDA, intellectual disability, learning differences, sensory processing differences, developmental delay, and complex neurodevelopmental presentations. It also supports families, including grandparents, foster carers, kinship carers, guardians, and extended family members, as well as professionals such as teachers, education support staff, disability support workers, and community support personnel.

### NDIS Information
Many Academy programs are designed to support family and carer capacity building and may be relevant to NDIS participants seeking disability-related education and support aligned with their NDIS goals, particularly through parent and carer training pathways. The NDIS Pricing Schedule includes specific parent and carer training and capacity-building support items.

### Learning Outcomes
Academy programs aim for meaningful improvement in everyday life. Participants build knowledge of emotional regulation and behaviour support; skills to reduce family stress, support regulation, build independence, improve communication, and strengthen participation; and confidence in advocacy, decision making, and working with schools and service providers. Programs include guides, templates, workbooks, action plans, and implementation tools.

### Standalone Webinars
In addition to Academy Membership, the practice runs standalone online webinars, such as one on supporting emotional regulation in children, priced from $99 AUD. These typically include replay access for 12 months, slides, a resource pack, and a completion certificate.

### Academy Pricing
Single masterclass access starts from $99 AUD per masterclass, with live or recorded access. Academy Membership is $499 AUD per year for unlimited access to all Academy content, including the recordings archive, new content releases, and member-only resources. An Intensive Workshop is $199 AUD per workshop, including a workbook and action plan resources. Organisation packages for schools, disability service providers, community organisations, and allied health teams are custom priced.

### Academy FAQ

#### Do I need to be on the NDIS to join the Academy?
No. The Academy is available to families, carers, educators and professionals worldwide.

#### Can I access recordings if I miss a live event?
Yes. Most Academy programs include recording access for flexible learning.

#### Is the Academy therapy?
No. The Academy provides psychoeducation, training and skill development. It does not provide psychological assessment, diagnosis, treatment or crisis support.

#### Can teachers and professionals enrol in the Academy?
Yes. Many Academy programs are suitable for education, disability and health professionals.

#### Are certificates provided for Academy programs?
Yes. Certificates of completion are available for most Academy programs.

#### Is the Academy content suitable for adults?
Yes. Many Academy topics are relevant across childhood, adolescence and adulthood.
