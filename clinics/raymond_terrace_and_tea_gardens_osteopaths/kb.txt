# Raymond Terrace and Tea Gardens Osteopaths — Myall Massage Clinic & Progressive Osteopathics

## About the Practice

Myall Massage Clinic and Progressive Osteopathics offer over 30 years of experience providing expert, hands-on treatment. The team includes qualified osteopaths and massage therapists who treat stress, tension, headaches, back pain, and muscular problems at two convenient locations in Tea Gardens and Raymond Terrace (Mid North Coast NSW).

Health fund approved. HICAPS and EFTPOS available. Wheelchair ramp and disabled toilet on site.

## Services

### Osteopathy
Safe, effective, hands-on osteopathic treatment for joint, neck, and back pain. Accepts EPC/Medicare care plans, DVA, and compensation claims.

### Remedial Massage
Complementary therapy targeting damaged, tense, or immobile muscles. Useful for problems affecting the muscles, tendons, and bones.

### Lymphatic Drainage
Specialised technique for treatment of Lymphoedema and fluid accumulation, including post-surgery swelling (e.g. after breast cancer surgery) and certain heart or medication-related conditions.

### Hot Stone Massage
Combines standard massage techniques with heated stones placed on specific body parts. Promotes blood flow, releases toxins, and creates deeper muscle relaxation. Stones placed over acupressure points support the body's own healing process.

### Myofascial Release
Hands-on technique to address myofascial restrictions that cause pain and limit movement.

### Dry Needling
Available as part of the treatment offering.
