# Halaxy Test Clinic

## About the clinic
Halaxy Test Clinic is a test home-visit clinic used to check the visit-address booking flow. It has no website and no physical premises: every appointment is a home visit, where a practitioner travels to the patient's address.

## Services
All appointments are home visits:
- Initial Assessment, 90 minutes
- Standard, 60 minutes
- Follow Up, 30 minutes
- Massage, 60 minutes
- Wound Care, 45 minutes

## Service area and practitioners
Each practitioner covers certain postcodes. Which practitioner can visit depends on the visit address, so the booking process asks the caller to confirm the address on file or give a new one.

## Contact
This is a test clinic. For anything this knowledge base does not cover, the caller can leave a message and the team will follow up.
