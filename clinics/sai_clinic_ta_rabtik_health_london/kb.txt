### Privacy
Personal data collected for bookings (name, contact details, appointment details, payment information, and medical history where applicable) is handled securely in compliance with UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and PECR. Data may be shared with Cliniko for appointment management and with secure payment processors. Clients can request access to, correction of, or deletion of their data, and can withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

### Cancellation and Payment Policy
24-hour notice is required for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows may result in a 50 percent cancellation fee. Payments must be made at the time of booking or after the session. Accepted payment methods are cash, card payments, and bank transfers. Clients using health insurance must confirm coverage in advance. No refunds are given for completed treatments.

### Medical Disclaimer

## Physiotherapy Services
Physiotherapy treatments are offered as personalized, home-based (domiciliary) care in the comfort of the client's home, designed to reduce pain, enhance mobility, and support recovery — whether the client is recovering from an injury or managing a long-term condition.

#### Sports Physiotherapy
Aids injury prevention, rehabilitation, and performance enhancement, helping athletes recover and improve flexibility, strength, and mobility. Typically 4 to 6 sessions.

#### Integrated Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy
Treats pain and improves mobility with personalized care. Typically 4 to 8 sessions.

#### Ultrasound Therapy
Uses high-frequency sound waves to reduce pain, promote healing, and improve mobility. Typically 4 to 8 sessions; acute conditions improve faster, chronic issues may require more treatment.

#### Acupuncture / Dry Needling
Uses fine needles to stimulate specific points in the body, promoting healing, pain relief, and energy flow. Typically 3 to 12 sessions.

## Ayurvedic Therapies

#### Kizhi (Herbal Poultice Massage)
Uses warm herbal poultices to target inflammation, muscle stiffness, and joint pain. 7 to 14 sessions recommended, especially for those recovering from injury, suffering from arthritis, or experiencing chronic muscle discomfort.

#### Januvasthi (Knee Pain Relief Therapy)
Targets knee discomfort by retaining warm medicated oil over the joints, relieving pain, stiffness, and arthritis symptoms while enhancing mobility. 7 to 15 sessions recommended.

#### Agnikarma (Pain Relief Heat Therapy)
Uses controlled heat to relieve chronic pain, inflammation, and joint stiffness. 5 to 10 sessions recommended, assisting in long-term pain management and mobility restoration.

#### Kativasthi (Lower Back Oil Therapy)
Uses warm herbal oil to relieve chronic lower back pain, sciatica, and stiffness. 7 to 10 sessions recommended, providing long-term pain management and mobility restoration.

#### Nasyakarma (Nasal Detox Therapy)
A nasal therapy that clears sinuses, improves respiratory function, and enhances mental focus. 7 to 14 sessions recommended, ideal for treating allergies, sinusitis, and migraines.

#### Nabhivasthi (Navel Therapy)
Applies warm oil around the navel area to promote digestion, relieve bloating, and balance gut health. 5 to 10 sessions recommended.

#### Lepa (Herbal Paste Therapy)
An herbal paste application that detoxifies the skin, reduces inflammation, and promotes healing. 5 to 10 sessions recommended, beneficial for muscle pain, swelling, and skin conditions.

#### Akshitarpana (Eye Rejuvenation Therapy)
Involves pooling medicated ghee around the eyes, relieving strain, dryness, and fatigue, while improving vision and overall eye health. 7 to 12 sessions recommended.

## Ayurvedic Massage

#### Shirodhara (Calming Oil Therapy)
Gently pours warm herbal oil over the forehead, reducing anxiety, promoting relaxation, and improving mental clarity. 5 to 7 sessions recommended, ideal for stress management and improving sleep.

#### Abhyanga (Full Body Oil Massage)
A deeply relaxing full-body warm oil massage that enhances circulation, reduces muscle tension, and promotes detoxification. 5 to 10 sessions recommended, improving vitality, reducing stress, and nourishing the skin.

#### Shiroabhyanga (Indian Head Massage)
A calming scalp, neck, and shoulder massage that improves blood circulation, alleviates headaches, and promotes mental clarity. 5 to 7 sessions recommended, for stress relief, migraine management, and nourishing the hair.
