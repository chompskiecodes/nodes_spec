# Portside Sports Physiotherapy

## About the Practice
### Overview
Portside Sports Physiotherapy provides honest, thorough and evidence-based physiotherapy in Newcastle. The practice has decades of combined experience with some of Australia's top athletes and sporting teams. Management is tailored to your body and goals, and only treatment that you actually need is recommended. The practice is fitted with elite strengthening equipment and measurement technologies.

## Who We See
### Private Clients
No referral required. HICAPS is available for immediate private health claims.
### Workers Compensation and CTP
A valid claim number is required.
### DVA Clients
DVA clients are seen.
### NDIS Participants
Self managed and plan managed NDIS participants are seen.
### GP Chronic Condition Management Plan Patients
Patients with a GP Chronic Condition Management Plan (GPCCMP) may be eligible for 5 Medicare subsidised physiotherapy appointments. A referral from your GP is required.

## The Portside Physio Team
### Phil Bosson
#### Role and Qualifications
Practice Principal. Titled APA Sports and Exercise Physiotherapist. Bachelor of Physiotherapy from the University of Newcastle, achieving Deans Merit Award. Masters of Sports and Exercise Physiotherapy from La Trobe University.
#### Experience
Phil established Portside Sports Physiotherapy after working in professional sport with the Newcastle Knights, including 5 seasons as Head Physiotherapist. He has experience managing acute and chronic sports injuries, rehabilitating athletes post-surgery and developing strength and running programs. He enjoys managing contact and game related injuries including ACL and MCL tears, hamstring and calf injuries, shoulder dislocations and ankle sprains, and diagnosing and managing long term niggling complaints.
#### Availability
Phil is currently unavailable for new patients and initial consultations.
