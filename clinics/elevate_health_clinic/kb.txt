## Dynamic Resilience System (DRS)
### How the Approach Works
A structured five-stage approach with Chiropractic, Physiotherapy and Exercise Physiology working together, one team and one plan. You move on when you meet clear milestones, not after a set number of weeks, and you leave with your own program.
#### Stage 1 Understand the Cause (Review)
A full assessment of your injury, movement, history and goals, including full musculoskeletal assessment, movement, strength and neural testing, and a written plan.
#### Stage 2 Settle Pain, Get Moving (Restore)
Hands-on care to ease pain and restore movement, with gentle exercise from day one: manipulation and mobilisation, soft tissue therapy and dry needling, and early range-of-motion exercise.
#### Stage 3 Strength and Capacity (Rebuild)
Progressive exercise to rebuild strength, control and load tolerance, guided by objective VALD testing.
#### Stage 4 Back to What You Love (Reignite)
Getting back to sport, the gym, work or everyday life, with return-to-sport or work testing, conditioning and performance programs, and load management.
#### Stage 5 Yours for the Long Term (Resilience)
Your own exercise program, self-management strategies and periodic progress reviews.
### Who Is Involved at Each Stage
Chiropractic: Review and Restore. Physiotherapy: Review, Restore and Rebuild. Exercise Physiology: Rebuild, Reignite and Resilience. Remedial Massage: Restore.
### DRS FAQ
#### Do I have to go through every stage?
No. Some people reach their goals by the end of Restore. Others, like athletes after performance, may start at Rebuild. Your practitioner will tell you where you're starting and why.
#### How long does it take?
It depends on you, your injury and your goals. You move on when you meet clear milestones, not after a set number of weeks.
#### Which practitioners will I see?
Usually a mix of Chiropractic, Physiotherapy and Exercise Physiology, depending on your needs. Your practitioners share notes and work from one plan.
#### How do I start?
Book a first appointment and we'll start with the Review stage. No referral needed.

Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or move an appointment. No fee for 2 hours' notice or more, though we ask for 24 hours where you can. Less than 2 hours' notice is a late cancellation: 50% of the appointment fee. No notice or no-show: the full appointment fee is charged.
### How to Cancel or Reschedule
Call your clinic (Bella Vista, Earlwood or Mobile EP), or use the link in your confirmation email or SMS to cancel or change. Email info@elevatehealthclinic.com.au for questions.
### Emergencies and Illness
If something genuinely outside your control stops you attending, let us know as soon as you can and we'll look at it case by case.
