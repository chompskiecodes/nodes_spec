## Services

### Remedial Massage

Remedial massage is a complementary therapy which aims to treat muscles that are damaged, tense or immobile. It is useful for a number of problems that affect the muscles, tendons and bones.

### Lymphatic Drainage

Lymphatic drainage is a technique developed in Germany for the treatment of lymphoedema, an accumulation of fluid that can occur after lymph nodes have been removed during surgery, most often in breast cancer. Certain heart conditions or medications may also affect the lymphatic system.

### Hot Stone Massage

During a hot stone massage, the therapist applies standard massage techniques while also placing hot stones on specific parts of the body. The warmed stones promote better blood flow, release toxins, and create deeper muscle relaxation than standard massage. Placing warmed stones over acupressure points helps release the flow of energy and promote the body's own healing process.
