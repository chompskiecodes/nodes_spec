# Shire Osteopath

## Practitioner

#### Kate Major, Osteopath (MOst., BAppSci., BSc)

Kate Major is a qualified and experienced osteopath based in Caringbah in the Sutherland Shire. She graduated with a Master of Osteopathy in 2014 and worked in Auckland before relocating to Sydney. She is AHPRA registered and a member of Osteopathy Australia. Additional training includes GEMt dry needling and pre- and post-natal care.

Kate treats teenagers through to older adults across a wide range of lifestyles and occupations, including tradies, office workers, hairdressers, police and defence force personnel, retirees, pregnant individuals, HSC and university students, high-level athletes, and those just getting into exercise.

## Location

Wellshare clinic rooms, Level 1, 416 Kingsway, Caringbah NSW 2229, above Shire Doctors and Dentists. Free parking is available on site and on surrounding streets.

## Hours

Monday: by appointment. Tuesday: closed. Closed on major holidays.

## Conditions Treated

Kate is trained to assess and treat a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions including neck pain and back pain, sciatica, arthritis, fibromyalgia, frozen shoulder, bursitis, tendinopathies (sprains and tears), pregnancy-related pelvic pain, muscle strains and tears, postural dysfunctions, headaches, sports injuries, joint pain, and chronic pain.

## Appointments

#### First Appointment

60 minutes. Upon booking you will receive a confirmation email with location, parking, and clinic access details, plus an online patient intake form to complete before your visit.

#### Subsequent Appointments

40 minutes.

#### What to Bring

Private health fund card, any relevant imaging results (x-ray, ultrasound), Medicare card if you have a chronic disease management plan from your GP, and comfortable clothing such as sports shorts and a t-shirt.

No referral is required to book an appointment.

## Fees and Rebates

Private health fund rebates apply. Medicare rebates are available under a chronic disease management plan (a GP referral is required for the plan itself, not for the osteopathy appointment).
