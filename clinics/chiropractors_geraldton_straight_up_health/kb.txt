## NEW PATIENT INFORMATION
### Personal Health History Form
After booking, patients complete the Personal Health History Form so the clinic can learn more about you and get a better picture about how you are feeling and what else you are dealing with in your life.
#### What the Form Covers
The form asks for your name, address, mobile, email, date of birth, gender, emergency contact name and phone number, and who referred you. It asks what brings you in today, your symptoms, how intense they are (from no pain to worst possible pain), what they feel like, and how they interfere with your work life, exercise, recreation, sleep, self-care, energy, attitude, patience, productivity and creativity. It asks how committed you are to correcting the issue and your immediate, short term and long term health goals. It asks about your children (how many, their names, ages and health concerns) and any current pregnancy (due date, number of past pregnancies and health concerns during this pregnancy). It includes a checklist of health history conditions and asks about allergies, medications, supplements, motor vehicle accidents, work-related and sports-related incidents or injuries, fractures or scars, and operations with approximate dates. It also asks about previous chiropractic care, including the name of the chiropractor, location, date of your last adjustment and when x-rays were last taken.

## CONDITIONS TREATED BY LIFESTYLE AND OCCUPATION
### Fishing, Farming and Fixing Machinery
#### Lower Back
Aside from known injuries to your lower back, you may also be standing for long periods in incorrect footwear. You are likely bent over for a good part of the day and pulling and pushing to get things done.
#### Neck and Upper Back
You work physically for long periods of time and often in cramped positions. This can cause extreme forces through your neck and upper back.
#### Knee and Foot
Knee and foot problems are often linked together. You may not have any pain in your foot, but when the bones are not moving well, it affects how you walk and this affects your knees. Knee problems can be caused by traumatic incidents, but knee and foot pain is often made worse by sitting still for too long, climbing stairs or ladders, or walking or standing in incorrect footwear.
#### Shoulder and Elbow
You may have had a specific injury such as pulling a load or getting an unexpected jolt. You perform repetitive actions such as pulling on a bar to get in and out of a vehicle or using your tools.
### Desk Jobs and Using Technology
#### Neck and Upper Back
You may be sitting in slumped postures for long periods of time. Your forward head position and incorrect posture cause extreme forces through your neck and upper back.
#### Headaches and Jaw
Sitting for long periods in incorrect positions can cause tightening through the muscles in the neck and jaw. You may clench your teeth when you use the computer.
#### Repetitive Strain
Carpal tunnel and tennis elbow are problems often reported by people using computers for long periods of time. This is due to poor posture, ergonomics and repetitive movements.
#### Lower Back
You may be sitting for too long or have poor ergonomics at the computer. Sitting with legs crossed over or under also puts excessive strain on the low back.
### Education and Child Care
#### Lower Back
You often stand for long periods in incorrect footwear. You may also be sitting in small chairs or going from sitting on the floor to standing frequently.
#### Neck, Upper Back and Jaw
You may be clenching your teeth or sitting for long periods with poor posture when marking papers, using computers or doing lesson planning. Your hectic schedule combined with poor posture results in extreme forces through your neck and upper back.
#### Shoulders, Elbows and Wrists
You perform repetitive motions all day, as well as lifting and carrying. These activities cause stress on your shoulders and elbows and wrists.
### Family

#### General Family Wellness

Shoulder and elbow pain can be brought on through a specific injury such as pulling a load or getting an unexpected jolt. You perform repetitive actions such as pulling on a bar to get in and out of a vehicle as well as using tools and controls repetitively.

You may sit in cramped positions, climb in and out of vehicles and jump to the ground. Knee problems can be caused by traumatic incidents, but knee and foot pain are often made worse by sitting still for too long, climbing stairs or ladders, or walking or standing in incorrect footwear.

### Healthy Ageing
#### Pain and Stiffness
It is easy to blame age for your aches and pains and most people think there is nothing that can be done. At Chiropractors Geraldton - Straight Up Health, we know you can feel so much more comfortable and flexible with some regular gentle care.
