The Yarrawonga Osteopathic Clinic has been known and trusted by locals for over 20 years.

Our team offers some of the best osteopathy services in the area. From back pain to sporting related injuries, our dedicated team are here to help you!

Under the careful guidance of Dr. Frank O’Dwyer, our practitioners work with you, using a range of techniques and procedures, to deliver the highest quality care and comfortable patient experience. Our friendly practitioners are fully qualified and undergo constant professional development.

We treat a range of ailments and issues at the clinic, here are just a few of the things we can help you with:

Back and neck pain

Sciatica

Headaches & migraines

Muscle strain, injuries, tendinitis

Arthritis

Work-related and repetitive strain injuries
