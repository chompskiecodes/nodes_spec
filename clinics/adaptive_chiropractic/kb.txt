# Adaptive Chiropractic

## Chiropractic Care
### What Chiropractic Care Addresses
The nervous system, protected by the spine, controls every cell, tissue, and organ, coordinating movement and the body's response to stress. Misalignments in the spine, known as subluxations, can disrupt nervous system function and affect health and wellbeing. Chiropractic care aims to keep the spine aligned so the nervous system functions optimally, relieving pain and supporting the body's natural healing process.
