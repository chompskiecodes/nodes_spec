# DC Sport Physio Clinic

## About The Clinic
### Overview
DC Sport Physio Clinic is a sports physiotherapy clinic in Thornhill, Ontario offering onsite, home care, and tele rehab physiotherapy services in the Greater Toronto Area. The clinic treats a variety of musculoskeletal conditions in children, athletes, office workers, and seniors. It focuses on getting results to improve function, strength, and eliminate pain, using evidence based treatment. Treatment methods are manual therapy, McKenzie Diagnostic Therapy, Shockwave, acupuncture, exercise therapy, and soft tissue release. Appointments can be booked by phone or online. The clinic has successfully treated over 500 patients in Thornhill and the GTA.

## Physiotherapy
### Initial Assessment
On the initial assessment there is a 40 minute assessment of movement, range, posture, and function. Patients should wear appropriate clothing for assessing movement, such as a T shirt and shorts. Treatment and exercises to help improve function follow the assessment. Exercise sheets are emailed after appointments to guide home treatment.
### Treatment Methods
The clinic has registered physiotherapists who are trained in manual therapy, acupuncture, exercise therapy, McKenzie Diagnostic Therapy, and soft tissue release.

## Tele Rehab
### About Tele Rehab
Telerehab is a safe and effective method of delivering physiotherapy assessment and treatment via videoconferencing using a webcam. It can be performed on a computer or laptop, tablet, or smart phone. Patients can securely share health information with the physiotherapist and be treated for their condition through special tests and exercises. Simple household objects can be used for exercises. Telerehab can be administered anywhere with a secure internet connection, and treatment should be done in a quiet and safe area according to each patient's needs.
### Who Should Use Tele Rehab
Telerehab can be used by anyone who cannot leave their home or office for a certain reason. Examples are pandemic, work travel, stay at home parent, sick and unable to leave home, living in a rural area or areas restricted to healthcare, or incarceration.
### Getting Started With Tele Rehab
Each patient should have a well lit area to work in and adequate room to move around. Patients can wear loose gym clothing they are able to move around in and roll up as needed for assessment. A sturdy chair with a back rest is recommended. The physiotherapist will make suggestions on what else can be used for treating the patient. An electronic device with webcam and microphone is required for telerehab.

## Concussion Treatment
Concussion is a form of brain injury caused by trauma. Symptoms involve dizziness, headaches, fogginess, and nausea. Physiotherapy can assess and treat symptoms associated with concussions. Treatment methods such as manual therapy, McKenzie Diagnostic Technique, acupuncture, exercise, and education can help significantly improve function. The physiotherapists use evidence based training to treat concussions.

## Shockwave Therapy
Shockwave therapy uses mechanical percussion to remodel and provide healing to chronic injuries and scar tissue. It has been shown to be very effective for long persisting injuries such as tennis elbow, tendon or ligament injuries, surgical scars, osteoarthritis, and frozen shoulder. For any injury, 3 sessions minimum are required. It is not safe for use over recent fracture sites or open surgical wounds.

## Home Care Physiotherapy
The clinic offers home visits for patients who are unable to come to the clinic, such as those with recent surgery, bedridden, in a wheelchair, in long term care, or needing extra specialized care. The first appointment involves a home assessment and creating a treatment plan. Followup appointments are done by a physiotherapist or physiotherapy assistant.

## Clinical Registered Massage Therapy
Clinical Registered Massage Therapy is used to assess and treat musculoskeletal and fascial issues affecting the body. It consists of treatment while clothed combining gentle movements, mobilizations, stretching, and touch. Exercises are included to help improve function. Massage therapy helps to improve blood flow, reduce pain, improve posture, improve range, relax muscles, and improve sport performance and daily function.
