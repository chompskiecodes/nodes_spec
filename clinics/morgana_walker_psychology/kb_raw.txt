Why choose

Choosing a psychologist is a deeply personal decision. You’re not just looking for expertise—you’re looking for someone who gets it. Someone who listens, understands, and works alongside you in a way that feels safe, respectful, and genuinely helpful. At Morgana Walker Psychology, care is thoughtful, collaborative, and grounded in real life.

Neuro‑affirming, strengths-based approach

You are not a problem to be fixed.
This practice is built on the belief that neurodiversity is a natural and valuable part of human experience. Rather than focusing on deficits, we work to understand how your mind works—so you can build on your strengths, navigate challenges, and feel more confident in yourself.

Deep understanding of ADHD & neurodiversity

Support is informed, practical, and grounded in lived experience.
Whether you’re exploring ADHD, supporting a neurodivergent child, or making sense of your own experience, you’ll receive clear, evidence-informed guidance that translates into everyday life—not just theory.

Genuine, human connection

Feeling safe and understood matters.
Sessions are warm, respectful, and collaborative. You’ll be met with curiosity, compassion, and honesty, not judgement. There’s space to be yourself, at your own pace.

Practical support that actually helps

Insight is important—but so is what comes next.
You’ll leave with:
- Strategies you can use right away
- A clearer understanding of what’s happening for you (or your child)
- Support that fits your real-world context

Whole-person, whole-family care

You don’t exist in isolation—and neither should your support.
Whether working with children, adults, or families, care is designed to support the whole system around you—including parents, relationships, learning environments, and daily life.

A space to feel understood, not judged

Sometimes the most powerful part of therapy is simply being seen.
This is a space where you can:
- Ask questions
- Explore without pressure
- Make sense of things in your own way

How to Build a Calm Down Corner at Home

An Online Webinar with Morgana Walker MAPS

Children experience big emotions every day, and learning to manage those emotions is a skill that develops over time. This practical and evidence-based webinar teaches parents, carers, and educators how to create a Calm Down Corner at home to support emotional regulation, resilience, and wellbeing.

Presented by
, this webinar explores the science behind emotional regulation, the role of co-regulation, and how a thoughtfully designed calm space can help children feel safe, supported, and able to develop lifelong coping skills.

Participants will learn:

What emotional regulation is and why it matters

How children’s brains and nervous systems respond to stress

The importance of co-regulation and connection

How to create an effective Calm Down Corner at home

Recommended sensory tools and calming activities

Common mistakes to avoid

How to support neurodivergent children and individual sensory needs

Common questions

Frequently asked
questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch at hello@morganawalker.com and we'll be happy to help.

Do I need to be on the NDIS?

No. The Academy is available to families, carers, educators and professionals worldwide.

Can I access recordings if I miss a live event?

Yes. Most Academy programs include recording access for flexible learning.

Is this therapy?

No. The Academy provides psychoeducation, training and skill development. It does not provide psychological assessment, diagnosis, treatment or crisis support.

Can teachers and professionals enrol?

Absolutely. Many Academy programs are suitable for education, disability and health professionals.

Are certificates provided?

Yes. Certificates of completion are available for most Academy programs.

Is the content suitable for adults?

Yes. Many Academy topics are relevant across childhood, adolescence and adulthood.
