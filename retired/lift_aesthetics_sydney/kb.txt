## Morpheus8 Skin Tightening & Remodelling
Morpheus8 is a microneedling device that uses radiofrequency to remodel and contour the face and body via subdermal adipose remodelling. This ablative fractional treatment penetrates deep into the skin and targets fat to contract and dissolve, producing a smoother, sleeker appearance. It suits skin rejuvenation, contouring, cellulite, stretch marks, scars, hyperhidrosis, and skin laxity of the stomach or knees.

#### Treatment Areas
Any area that can benefit from skin renewal, including the lower and upper eyelids, smile lines, forehead, and cheeks — virtually any area showing wrinkles, discoloration, or acne scars.

#### How Many Treatments Are Needed
A monthly treatment is recommended, since it takes about 4 weeks to form new collagen. A minimum series of 3 treatments is generally required, depending on the concern.

#### How Long Results Last
New collagen and elastin take about 4 weeks to form; the skin continues remodelling in the subcutaneous layer for up to 3 months. A second treatment can be booked once remodelling is complete to build on results. Maintenance is generally recommended every 12–18 months.

#### Comfort & Pain
The clinic uses prescription-grade numbing cream and offers "Happy Gas" to help with anxiety or discomfort during treatment.

#### Why Adjustable Needle Depth Matters
A trained practitioner adjusts needle depth to build a treatment plan customised to each patient. Common goals include improving skin texture, supporting skin tightening and tone, refining pores, and stimulating and banking collagen and elastin at depth — across the eyes, face, jawline, neck, lower face, abdomen, cellulite-prone areas, stretch marks, buttocks, and intimate wellness areas. As skin ages, the fibroseptal network deteriorates, causing excess skin with loss of support (visible via a skin pinch test); Morpheus8 stimulates collagen and elastin to tighten and improve skin tone.

#### Downtime
Expect swelling, redness, and possible spot bruising for 3–5 days, since the treatment causes controlled injury to stimulate collagen and elastin. Specific aftercare products are used for the first 3 days; sunscreen and mineral make-up can be applied from day 4. Skin may flake around days 4–5 and typically returns to normal by days 5–7. Some patients — especially on the neck — form crusts during healing; these should be left to fall off naturally, with hydrating products used to support healing.

## Forma — Women's Health (Vaginal Rejuvenation)
Forma (by InMode) uses bipolar RF electrothermal heat via an intravaginal handpiece to improve mucosal tightening, correct vaginal pH, and support vaginal tightening and rejuvenation. It is also recommended for younger patients experiencing vaginal dryness from pH changes (for example from the contraceptive pill or antibiotics). The minimally invasive bipolar RF mechanism heats tissue to a controlled 40–45°C, triggering an inflammatory cascade that initiates new collagen, blood vessel, and elastin formation for a skin-tightening effect.

#### Recommended For
Abnormal vaginal pH (recurrent discharge with no identified cause), postpartum recovery, scarring after delivery, vaginal rejuvenation, or preventive care. It can also help with genital itch, incontinence, and other genito-urinary symptoms experienced across different phases of womanhood.

#### Treatment Details
A course of 3 monthly sessions is recommended, followed by yearly maintenance. Each session takes about 20 minutes.

#### Contraindications
Pacemaker, abnormal vaginal bleeding, or pelvic cancer.

#### Anaesthesia & Pain
No anaesthesia is needed. Discomfort is minimal — often described as similar to a warm bath.

#### Post-Procedure Care
Nothing should be inserted into the vagina for the next day. Patients may feel warmth in the treated area that evening, and can apply ice if needed.

## Aviva RFAL — Non-Surgical Body & Intimate Contouring
Aviva RFAL (radiofrequency-assisted lipolysis) uses a non-aspirating cannula to dissolve fat, tighten skin, and contract the fibroseptal network from within, with collagen and elastin stimulation for intimate wellness and general body contouring. Some patients use it as a first step toward labial puffing. It is a non-surgical, minimally invasive procedure performed under local anaesthesia in-clinic; compression garments are worn for a few days afterward to reduce swelling. Scheduling after a period or mid-cycle is recommended to avoid fluid retention around period time. It is performed once only, though combination treatments with prescription medication or other energy devices may be recommended over time as collagen, elastin, and fat naturally continue to change with age.

Labial puffing is a separate, non-surgical procedure that adds volume, hydration, and fullness to the labia majora using prescription injections or fat transfer from elsewhere on the body, addressing concerns such as pain during intercourse, dryness, and irritation caused by volume loss from ageing, weight loss, or childbirth.

#### Expected Side Effects
Swelling, discomfort, bruising, blisters (in patients prone to shingles), redness, and moistness around the injection site are common for a few hours to overnight. Some patients — particularly if treated during their period — develop persistent swelling, which resolves fully over time. Scarring and temporary numbness or tingling from nerve irritation are rare.

#### How Aviva Differs From Liposuction
Liposuction is a surgical procedure requiring more time, cost, and recovery. Aviva RFAL is an aspiration-free, cannula-based procedure that gently heats and remodels tissue at a subcutaneous level rather than suctioning fat out, resulting in a less invasive procedure with a lower risk of secondary skin laxity than removing large fat volumes surgically.

#### Areas Treated
Assessed on a case-by-case basis after consultation. Aviva has been used across delicate areas including the external genital area, to address skin laxity and textural changes — for example after childbirth, ageing, or weight fluctuation.

#### Suitable Candidates
Patients seeking a minimally invasive way to improve comfort or appearance of the external genitals without surgery — typically addressing skin laxity, soft tissue changes, or comfort/confidence concerns. An initial consultation is required before any procedure is booked.

#### Results & Recovery
Improvements in skin tightness and contour are typically noticeable shortly after treatment and continue to develop over the following weeks and months. Recovery is generally quicker than surgical alternatives, given the very small entry points and local anaesthesia; mild swelling, tenderness, or sensitivity usually resolves within a few days.

#### Longevity
Results are designed to be similar to surgical outcomes, though longevity depends on the individual's skin condition and the natural ageing process. Age, childbirth, weight change, and menopause can reduce results over time; combining Aviva with supportive treatments such as fillers can extend results for years.

#### Cost
Pricing varies by individual anatomy, treatment complexity, and aesthetic goals, and is not listed online — an initial in-person consultation is required for a quote and treatment plan.

## EMS & VTone Muscle Toning
Electric Muscle Stimulation (EMS) uses electrical impulses to induce muscular contraction, strengthening weak muscles, reducing swelling, relieving pain, and supporting wound healing. Tone (EMS) is recommended for the abdomen, arms, legs, and buttocks. VTone (EMS) is designed for pelvic floor retraining — neuromuscular stimulation equivalent to thousands of Kegel exercises.

#### How It Works
Both devices use the same InMode EMS technology via different application methods, inducing deep, involuntary muscle contractions that support muscle recovery and can be used alone or alongside physical therapy. Tone targets key muscle groups such as the abdomen or buttocks to strengthen and firm underlying muscle. VTone delivers gentle EMS to the pelvic floor via an intravaginal device.

#### VTone For Pelvic Floor Health
VTone can help with urinary incontinence, urge incontinence, and muscle laxity, working with the body's natural healing and strengthening processes. Patients often experience improved pelvic floor responsiveness, comfort, and confidence over a series of sessions.

#### Suitable Candidates
Tone suits patients seeking a non-invasive way to improve muscle tone and core strength, particularly in the abdomen or buttocks. VTone suits patients specifically aiming to rehabilitate and strengthen the pelvic floor, including those experiencing muscle laxity from ageing, menopause, or postpartum changes. Both can be used independently or together; a full consultation is completed beforehand, and patients are encouraged to speak with their GP first.

#### Recommended Sessions
Most patients need around 6 to 8 sessions for optimal results, spaced about a week apart, with an individual treatment plan set at consultation. Follow-up maintenance appointments can also be arranged.

#### Comfort Level
Both treatments feel like strong but tolerable muscle contractions; intensity is adjusted for comfort throughout, and there is no downtime.

#### Postpartum Recovery
Both Tone and VTone are indicated for postpartum recovery once cleared by a GP — Tone rebuilds abdominal strength and deep core muscles, while VTone works on the pelvic floor, which can weaken during pregnancy and childbirth.

#### Downtime
None — patients return to their normal routine immediately after each session.

## Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP)
PRP is an injectable treatment using a person's own blood. Platelets are fundamental to tissue healing and new cell growth; PRP encourages collagen production, regenerates skin tissue, and reactivates the skin's own rejuvenation process. Released growth factors support healing, softening fine lines and wrinkles and improving skin tone and texture.

#### Administration
Blood is drawn from the patient into a syringe and spun in a centrifuge to separate its components. The resulting platelet-rich plasma is then reinjected and/or micro-needled back into the skin.

#### Duration
A PRP treatment takes about an hour in total, including roughly 30 minutes for blood draw, centrifuge processing, and topical numbing, with the remaining time for injection and/or micro-needling.

#### Discomfort
Mild burning or tingling may be felt during reinjection, lasting only a few minutes. Topical numbing cream and ice are used to reduce discomfort.

#### Results Timeline
Most patients notice tightening and a more even skin tone within one to two weeks, with results continuing to build for up to 6 months.

#### Longevity
Results can last for years, though longevity varies with the individual's natural plasma quality and lifestyle.

#### Areas Treated
Most commonly the face, neck, and scalp.

#### Downtime
None expected, though the treated skin may be red for 1–24 hours with some possible swelling; recovery varies by patient.

#### Number Of Treatments Needed
Results can appear after one treatment; 2–3 treatments are generally advised, though more may be recommended for some patients.

#### Risks & Side Effects
PRP is generally safe; the most common side effects are mild to moderate swelling and redness.

#### Who Should Not Have PRP
Patients with certain health conditions, and patients who are pregnant, are not candidates for PRP — suitability is confirmed at consultation.

## Prescription-Only Injectable Treatment (Muscle Relaxant)
This prescription-only treatment is one of the most cost-effective options in the aesthetics industry for improving skin quality and appearance. It is an acetylcholine release inhibitor and neuromuscular blocking agent that causes temporary muscle relaxation and atrophy (slimming) — choosing an experienced provider is key to a good result. Lift Aesthetics Sydney uses TGA-approved injectables, which work by blocking acetylcholine release to prevent muscle contraction; correct dosing and injection technique are essential to achieving the desired result.

#### What It's Used For
Temporarily preventing specific muscle movements — used for axillary hyperhidrosis (excess underarm sweating), platysmal bands, and, medically, for migraines and upper limb muscle spasticity.

#### How It Works
It blocks the release of membrane-bound acetylcholine transmitter at the neuromuscular endplate, causing temporary paralysis of the affected muscle.

#### Side Effects
Common: generalised weakness, eyelid drooping, fatigue, flu-like symptoms, pain, bruising, swelling, and reddening at the injection site. Less common: itching. Rare: skin rashes (including at the injection site) and muscle weakness.

#### Contraindications
Pregnancy, lactation, neuromuscular diseases such as Myasthenia Gravis or Eaton-Lambert syndrome, aminoglycoside antibiotics, allergies or hypersensitivity to botulinum toxin or human albumin, and infection at the injection site.

#### Administration Time
Generally 10 to 20 minutes, though clinic visits can vary.

#### Onset & Duration
Results begin within 1–3 days and reach full effect at 1–2 weeks. Effects generally last around 3–6 months.

## Biostimulators & Bio-Regenerators
Several TGA-approved biostimulators are available. These injectables gradually help replace lost collagen and elastin, supporting cells and tissue — stimulated fibroblasts form a scaffold that helps restore deep structure and volume. As the microparticles are absorbed, they stimulate collagen production, gradually restoring facial volume.

#### Areas Treated
PLLA (poly-L-lactic acid) is clinically approved to treat facial lipoatrophy through volume restoration, thickening the skin and filling sunken areas. It was first FDA-approved in 2004 for facial wasting from antiretroviral HIV therapy, and is also used for lipoatrophy in non-HIV patients. Treatment involves a series of injections and requires diligent post-treatment massage for the best result.

#### Results & Longevity
Full effect typically develops over an average of three monthly sessions, with subtle, gradual skin changes over a couple of months. Effects can last 18 to 24 months or more, though results are not permanent and vary by individual.

#### Safety & Side Effects
PLLA is FDA-approved in the US and TGA-approved in Australia, and has been used worldwide since 1999. It is a synthetic material absorbed naturally by the body with no human or animal components, so allergy testing isn't required beforehand. Possible side effects include redness, bruising, pain at the injection site, tenderness, itching, bleeding, swelling, and lumps (including larger lumps with or without inflammation) — patients experiencing side effects should speak to a health professional.
