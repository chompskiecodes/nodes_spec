Strength sessions

Our strength sessions are exercise scientist-led programs designed to improve strength, confidence, movement quality, and overall function. Each session is tailored to your goals, whether you are recovering from injury, managing a chronic condition, returning to sport, or wanting to build strength safely. We focus on correct technique, progressive loading, and long-term results to help you move better and feel stronger.

People recovering from injury or surgery, managing chronic pain or ongoing conditions, returning to sport or gym, or wanting a structured and supervised strength program.

Builds strength and confidence, improves movement and technique, supports injury prevention and rehabilitation, enhances physical performance, and helps you return to daily activities, sport, or exercise safely.

Clinical reformer pilates is a physiotherapist-led exercise program using a specialised reformer machine to improve strength, flexibility, posture, and movement control. unlike generic pilates classes, it’s tailored to your specific needs—often for rehabilitation, injury prevention, or performance enhancement. exercises are low-impact but highly targeted, making them suitable for all levels, from beginners to athletes.

People recovering from injury or surgery, manage chronic conditions

Strengthens core and stabilising muscles, improves posture and alignment, supports injury prevention and rehabilitation

Dry needling is a targeted treatment technique used by physiotherapists to help relieve muscle tightness, pain, and movement restrictions. It involves the use of fine, sterile needles placed into specific trigger points or tight areas of muscle to help reduce tension, improve mobility, and support recovery. Dry needling is often used alongside exercise, manual therapy, and rehabilitation to address the underlying cause of pain and improve long-term function.

People experiencing muscle tightness, trigger points, sports injuries, neck or back pain, headaches, overuse injuries, or reduced range of motion.

Helps reduce muscle tension and pain, improves mobility, supports injury recovery, and can assist with returning to normal movement, sport, and daily activities.

Where are you located?

We are located in Westmead and Leonay. Please head to our
locations
page to find out more, otherwise give us a call on 0405 088 137

How long are the sessions?

Initial assessment are from 30 minutes to 60 minutes depending on the injury. This is to allow for ample time to assess, diagnose, educate, treat, and establish a home exercise program. Subsequent assessments are 30 minutes to allow for treatment and home exercise program updates to be made.

What do I bring to my first appointment?

We are so pleased that you chose One Stop Rehab as your rehabilitation provider to get you back on track. You do not need a referral to attend physiotherapy as we are primary health providers.

Please bring the following:

Doctor’s referral (if applicable)

Any scans that you had (if applicable)

Appropriate loose fitting clothing for the movement assessment

If you are under WorkCover or CTP please bring your claim number, name of the insurance company, and any other information relating to your case manager

What are your fees?

We bulk bill under the EPC medicare program once a GP referral has been provided. For private patients or private health insurance holders, fees are $160 for initial appointments.

Do you have a cancellation policy?

Although we would love to help you on your journey, we understand that life can get in the way and so we kindly ask you to cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment by messaging 0405 088 137.

If you fail to attend your appointment without notice then you may be charged a fee of $30. The cancellation fee must be paid before any further appointments are made.
