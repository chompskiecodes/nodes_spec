# Angus Osteopathic and Sports Injury Clinics

## About the Clinic
Angus Osteopathic and Sports Injury Clinics provide osteopathy in Carnoustie and Montrose. Osteopaths are Allied Healthcare Professionals and primary care providers who work alongside physiotherapists in musculoskeletal roles within NHS England. They specialise in the diagnosis, treatment and management of pain and dysfunction arising from the musculoskeletal system.

The clinic offers an honest, evidence based, patient centred approach, combining a Bachelor of Science degree in Anatomy, Physiology and Osteopathy with more than 17 years of experience in private practice. The clinic specialises in the diagnosis and management of musculoskeletal problems including hip and knee osteoarthritis, shoulder problems such as rotator cuff tears and frozen shoulder, and low back and neck issues.

The clinic also offers scientifically based personal training sessions to improve mobility, strength, performance and overall wellbeing, and specialises in strength and conditioning for older adults to help reduce the effects of age related sarcopenia, osteopenia and osteoporosis, improving mobility, reducing falls and improving overall quality of life.

## Conditions Treated
The clinic treats low back pain, morning stiffness, neck pain, joint stiffness, trapped nerve, mobility problems, shoulder pain, difficulty getting out of a chair, tendonitis, pain on movement, pain while gardening and arthritic pain.

## Services

### Neck Pain Treatment
The clinic provides tailored therapy for neck pain, serving Carnoustie and the surrounding area including Arbroath, Monifieth and Broughty Ferry. Treatment combines an understanding of pain management with the anatomical and physiological principles of exercise therapy and rehabilitation, using cervical spine manipulation, soft tissue mobilisation, deep tissue massage, hot stone massage, dry cupping and passive stretching, together with postural advice and strengthening of supportive muscles.

The clinic provides pain relief for injury, dysfunction and muscle damage around the knee and hip, serving Carnoustie, Arbroath, Monifieth and Broughty Ferry. ### Back Pain Treatment
The clinic provides osteopathy for back pain in Carnoustie and the surrounding area, using hands-on techniques such as spinal manipulation, soft tissue release and joint mobilisation, together with tailored exercise regimens, ergonomic advice and lifestyle modifications to address the root causes of back pain.

## What to Expect

### Initial Consultation

#### Case History
The practitioner asks about the patient's symptoms and past medical history, and asks specific questions to help formulate a differential diagnosis.

#### Examination
The practitioner examines the affected area and related areas, asks the patient to perform simple movements, and carries out orthopaedic and neurological tests to confirm or rule out possible diagnoses.

#### Diagnosis
The practitioner explains, to the best of their ability, what is causing the patient's discomfort, including any differential diagnosis where the picture is not yet clear.

#### Treatment and Rehabilitation Plan
Treatment can include advice, reassurance and lifestyle changes as well as hands-on techniques, and the most appropriate treatment plan is discussed and carried out based on current evidence.

#### Advice and Reassurance
Patients are given specific advice on what to expect and what to do before the next session to support recovery and reduce symptoms.

### Follow-up Consultation
A follow-up appointment is required to review progress and guide future management. Symptoms are reviewed, the area is re-examined with specific tests, and appropriate treatment is carried out. Ongoing individual advice is given on avoiding symptoms in future, related to the patient's work, lifestyle and hobbies.

## Treatment Plans
Patients who invest in comprehensive treatment plans often experience improved outcomes through consistency and adherence to care. Treatment plans allow patients to buy a number of sessions at a discount. Most musculoskeletal conditions can be helped with appropriate personalised rehabilitation and time; some conditions, such as frozen shoulder, rotator cuff strains, tendinopathy or arthritis, require weeks or months of care.

One available plan is an initial consultation plus two follow-up sessions, allowing time to formulate a treatment plan and assess early results. A monthly personal training rehabilitation plan offers weekly, scientifically driven exercise physiology sessions that can help conditions including high blood pressure, COPD, pre-diabetes, general stiffness, weakness and balance issues.

All treatment blocks are valid for 3 months. Any refund is given at the full consultation price plus a £10 administration charge.

## Private Healthcare and Insurance
A GP referral is not required to see an osteopath. Some major insurance companies cover osteopathy; patients are advised to check their policy and contact their insurer to confirm whether a GP referral is needed to make a claim. Previous patients have used insurers including Aviva and Simply Health.
