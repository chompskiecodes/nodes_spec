# Cliff Villages Physiotherapy

## Location

Cliff Villages Physiotherapy sees patients by appointment at Walnut Place, Lower High Street, Waddington, Lincoln, Lincolnshire LN5 9QA, United Kingdom. Use the clinic calendar to check current availability.

## Physiotherapy and sports massage

Mathew Rudd, the founder, is a musculoskeletal physiotherapist. The clinic provides one-to-one physiotherapy assessment and treatment, including hands-on care, exercise and rehabilitation advice tailored to the patient. The website lists care for elbow, foot and ankle, and hip and groin problems as examples of physiotherapy. These are reasons for a physiotherapy visit.

## Appointments and fees

- Initial Assessment: 45 minutes for a first physiotherapy appointment. The published fee is £60.
- Follow Up: 30 minutes for a standard physiotherapy follow-up. The published fee is £50.
- Sports Massage: 30 minutes. Ask the clinic to confirm the fee.
- Extended follow-up: the website lists £60. The clinic arranges extended visits directly.
