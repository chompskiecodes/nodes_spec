# Mandy Venable RN, CFCS

## Who Needs Routine Foot Care
Routine foot care is recommended for diabetics, individuals with compromised circulation or sensation in their feet, and individuals who simply cannot reach or see their feet. Routine foot care can help manage corns, calluses, ingrown toenails, fissures, and dry cracked skin.

#### Examples
Toenails can become unmanageable with age, causing discomfort when left long. Bunions can cause toe overlap, making it difficult to manage toenails. Ingrown toenails occur when nails grow into the surrounding skin. Patients of all ages, not only older adults, may need assistance managing foot care.

## About The Service
Mandy Venable is a Registered Nurse (RN, CFCS) trained by a podiatrist to perform routine foot care for individuals who are not able to manage their own foot care. Service is provided compassionately in the comfort of the patient's home or at the clinic. Foot Logix pedicuetical products are used during service.
