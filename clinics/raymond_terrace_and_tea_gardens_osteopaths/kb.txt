# Raymond Terrace and Tea Gardens Osteopaths (Progressive Osteopathics)

## About the Practice

Progressive Osteopathics is a long-established osteopathic practice in Raymond Terrace, with 35 years of care in Raymond Terrace and 25 years in Tea Gardens. It is led by Bruce Sutton, who has practised osteopathy for over 35 years, alongside osteopath Elisa Brownhill. The approach is holistic and patient-centred, using gentle, hands-on techniques to diagnose, treat and prevent a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions. Patients of all ages are welcome, from babies to seniors.

## Services

### General Osteopathy
Holistic, hands-on treatment for musculoskeletal conditions using stretching, massage and mobilisation techniques to relieve pain and restore function.

### Back and Neck Pain
Treatment for disc injuries, sciatica and postural strain using manual techniques to reduce tension and improve joint movement.

### Headaches and Migraines
Gentle techniques targeting the neck, upper back and cranial structures for tension-type headaches and migraines.

### Sports Injuries
From acute sprains to chronic overuse injuries, helping athletes and active people recover and return to peak performance.

### Pregnancy and Post-natal
Safe, gentle treatment for pregnancy-related discomfort including back pain, pelvic girdle pain and post-natal recovery.

### Paediatric Osteopathy
Very gentle treatment for babies and children, including feeding difficulties, unsettledness and developmental concerns.

### Postural Correction
Postural assessment and tailored treatment plans including manual therapy, exercises and ergonomic advice.

### Arthritis and Joint Pain
Gentle management of arthritis symptoms to improve mobility, reduce stiffness and improve quality of life.

### Elderly Care
Gentle treatment to maintain mobility and independence, ease aches, improve balance and reduce fall risk.
