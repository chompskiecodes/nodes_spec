## About Rachael
Rachael is a qualified Myotherapist and Remedial Massage Therapist who treats musculoskeletal pain, including both acute and chronic conditions. Her approach focuses on postural dysfunctions as well as identifying patterns and imbalances that could be contributing to symptoms.

### Techniques Used
Rachael tailors each treatment to the individual and commonly uses active release techniques, deep tissue therapy, passive release techniques, cupping, and dry needling when appropriate.

### Conditions Commonly Treated
Rachael has a strong interest in posture-related pain and commonly treats neck and shoulder pain, headaches, back pain, hip and glute pain, postural-related dysfunction, and full body muscular tension including the scalp and feet.

### Background
Rachael has a background in beauty, hair, makeup, nails, remedial massage, and myotherapy, bringing a broad skill set and adaptable treatment style.

## Treatment Types & Pricing

### Choosing an Appointment Length
The right appointment length depends on what the patient is coming in for and how in-depth treatment needs to be. This can vary depending on how tight things are, whether other areas of the body are contributing, and how long the issue has been present. For new patients, it is recommended to book a 50 minute appointment to allow enough time to go through history, complete an assessment, and begin treatment.

### The Top Up - 25 Minutes - $90
Perfect for keeping things on track between appointments. Best suited for one main area or concern that needs a little extra attention, maintenance, or relief before it becomes an ongoing issue.

### The Regular - 50 Minutes - $125
A well-rounded treatment option suited to most people and most concerns. Great for general maintenance, ongoing aches and pains, or a couple of areas in one session.

### The Extended - 80 Minutes - $170
Designed for more in-depth treatment and complex presentations. Ideal for multiple areas of concern, longer-standing tension, postural or muscular imbalances, or when there is a more detailed medical history to consider.

## FAQ

#### What is myotherapy?
Myotherapy is a hands-on treatment that assesses and treats muscle pain, joint issues, and movement-related problems. It focuses on identifying contributing factors such as tight muscles, load, posture, and movement patterns.

#### What can I expect in my first appointment?
The first appointment includes a discussion about symptoms and history, an assessment of movement and contributing factors, and treatment based on what is appropriate on the day.
