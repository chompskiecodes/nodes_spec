## Services
### Manual Therapy
Manual therapy is about understanding why the body has become irritated, stiff or overloaded in the first place. Every treatment is tailored to the individual, combining hands-on osteopathic techniques with an understanding of biomechanics, movement and modern pain science. Treatment may include soft tissue massage, joint mobilisation, manipulation and movement-based techniques designed to reduce pain, restore mobility and help the body move more freely. Used for acute back pain, neck stiffness, headaches, shoulder issues and long-standing chronic pain.
### Dry Needling
Dry needling is used to reduce muscle tension, improve movement and calm irritated or overactive tissues. It is integrated into a broader treatment plan alongside hands-on therapy, movement rehabilitation and practical advice rather than used as a standalone treatment.
### Rehabilitation
Rehabilitation programs are designed to fit real life. Exercises are selected to improve strength, mobility, control and confidence without overwhelming busy schedules, using the smallest amount of exercise needed to create meaningful progress. Drawing from osteopathy, clinical pilates principles and modern rehabilitation approaches, programs are tailored to your goals, lifestyle and current capacity. Suitable for recovering from an injury, returning to exercise, managing persistent pain or wanting to move better.
### Running and Hiking Injuries and Prevention
Treatment combines hands-on therapy with an understanding of training loads, movement mechanics and rehabilitation. Common presentations include knee pain, achilles tendinopathy, plantar fasciitis, hip pain, lower back pain and overuse injuries associated with increasing mileage or returning from time away. Suitable for runners, hikers and endurance athletes, including those preparing for a first trail race or an ultramarathon.
### Orofacial Myology
Orofacial myology focuses on the function of the muscles of the face, jaw, tongue and airway, and how these influence breathing patterns, jaw pain, headaches, sleep quality, posture and overall function. This additional training complements the osteopathic approach, especially for jaw pain and TMJ dysfunction, headaches and neck tension, mouth breathing patterns, sleep-related concerns, and persistent facial or neck discomfort.
