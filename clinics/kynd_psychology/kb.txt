# KYND Psychology

## About

Navid Khorassani is a Clinical Psychologist and Psychoanalytic Psychotherapist providing psychotherapy for adults and young adults in Kew, serving clients from across Melbourne's inner-east including Balwyn, Hawthorn, Camberwell, and Richmond. Founder of KYND Psychology, his approach focuses on exploring the unconscious, emotional patterns and relational dynamics that shape a client's life, fostering a secure and non-judgmental environment to help clients navigate internal conflicts and build resilience.

### Professional Qualifications

Navid holds a Master of Clinical Psychology.

### Certifications

Navid is an AHPRA Registered Clinical Psychologist. Registration: Navid Mohtaj Khorassani, PSY0002873150.

### Experience

Practising clinically since 2018, Navid has extensive experience providing depth-oriented psychotherapy for adults and young adults facing complex psychological challenges. KYND Psychology operates from Kew, serving clients throughout Melbourne's inner-eastern suburbs.

### Approach

Navid's clinical work is grounded in classical and contemporary psychoanalytic theory, specifically the traditions of Freud and Lacan. He integrates evidence-based approaches when clinically appropriate, including Transference-Focused Psychotherapy (TFP), Mentalization-Based Therapy (MBT), and contemporary frameworks such as ACT and CBT, tailored to each client's needs.

### Specializations

Areas of expertise include depression, anxiety, complex trauma, grief and loss, and identity struggles, with particular focus on relationship difficulties and personality-related concerns addressed through both individual and couple-based interventions.

## Services

Individual and couple therapy sessions with a clinical psychologist are delivered in person from the Kew practice, close to Hawthorn, Camberwell, Balwyn, and Richmond.

### Individual Therapy

Individual psychotherapy sessions provide a confidential space to explore emotional difficulties, relational patterns, and personal challenges. Therapy addresses anxiety and depression, bipolar and mood regulation, PTSD and complex trauma, grief and loss, obsession and compulsion, panic attacks, phobias, eating disorders, body dysmorphia, borderline personality disorder, identity concerns, relationship and attachment difficulties, neurodiversity (ADHD and ASD), and personal development. Additional areas include major life transitions such as separation, migration, and career change, along with emotional regulation difficulties, chronic stress, and general life pressures.

### Couples Therapy

Couples therapy helps partners better understand their relationship dynamics and develop healthier patterns of communication and emotional connection. Common areas addressed include communication difficulties, conflict and recurring relationship patterns, trust, intimacy and attachment difficulties, relationship repair and rebuilding connection, infidelity and relationship betrayal, separation and relationship transitions, and pre-marital counselling.

### Referrals

A referral is not required to book an appointment at KYND Psychology. Clients can attend privately without a referral. A valid referral and Mental Health Treatment Plan are only required if a client wishes to claim Medicare rebates.

### Medicare Rebates

Clients referred by a GP or psychiatrist under a valid Mental Health Care Plan may be eligible to receive Medicare rebates for psychological treatment. As sessions are provided by a Clinical Psychologist, the current Medicare rebate is $149.05 per session. Eligible clients may access rebates for up to 10 sessions per calendar year, subject to Medicare requirements. Couples Therapy sessions are not eligible for Medicare rebates.

### Private Health Insurance

Clients whose extras cover includes psychology may be able to claim a rebate through their private health insurer. Eligibility and rebate amounts vary between providers and levels of cover, so clients should check directly with their insurer. The same session cannot be claimed through both Medicare and private health insurance.

### Session Fees

KYND Psychology is a private practice and does not offer bulk billing. Appointments are 50 minutes in duration. Payment is due on the day of the appointment unless alternative arrangements have been made in advance. After-hours appointments attract a higher consultation fee. For current fees, contact info@kyndpsychology.com.au.

### Cancellations and Missed Appointments

Clients are asked to give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or unattended appointments, incur the full consultation fee, as the appointment time has been reserved specifically for the client. Medicare rebates are not available for cancellation fees.
