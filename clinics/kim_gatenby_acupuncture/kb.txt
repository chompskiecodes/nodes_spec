# Kim Gatenby Acupuncture

## About Kim Gatenby

Kim Gatenby holds a Bachelor of Applied Science in Traditional Chinese Medicine (2007) and a Master of Reproductive Medicine with Excellence from the University of New South Wales (2022). She has 19 years of experience as a fully qualified acupuncturist. She also holds a Certificate in Heated Stone Massage and a Certificate in Sports Nutrition.

Kim is a member of ESHRE and AACMA, and is AHPRA registered.

Kim's main area of interest is reproductive health for women and men. She educates other practitioners in fertility care and has won awards for her presentations, including Best Presentation in Scientific/Academic Research at AACMAC 2022.

Kim previously owned a highly successful fertility acupuncture clinic in Sydney and has since relocated to the Mid North Coast of NSW to serve the Port Macquarie and Hinterland community.

## What to Expect at a Consultation

The initial consultation is comprehensive, covering health history, concerns, and goals. Kim reviews any results sent in advance and is ready to hear from the patient directly. Plenty of time is given for the patient to feel heard. Kim explains diagnoses and outlines a written treatment plan so patients do not need to memorise everything discussed. If acupuncture is included, it takes place during the same appointment while Kim prepares the written plan and any herbal medicine required.

Partners with fertility concerns can also attend. A partner with diagnosed male infertility requires a separate appointment. Patients leave with a clear, achievable plan.

## Fertility Care

### Overview

Kim Gatenby Acupuncture specialises in reproductive health, combining traditional Chinese medicine with modern reproductive science. Kim supports patients seeking natural conception and those undergoing IVF. She has been supporting fertility patients for over 18 years.

Conditions Kim treats include endometriosis, PCOS (also known as PMOS), thyroid dysfunction, recurrent miscarriage, male factor infertility, low ovarian reserve, NK cell-related fertility issues, and fragmented embryos.

### IVF Support and Demeter Fertility Partnership

Kim has partnered with Demeter Fertility in Sydney. Patients can begin their IVF or natural conception journey with Kim and maintain continuity of care throughout. Demeter offers ovarian rejuvenation (PRP) and IVF services. Consultations with the IVF doctor can be conducted via TeleHealth, meaning patients only need to travel to Sydney for egg collections or embryo transfers. This brings a holistic IVF option to the Mid North Coast.

### Natural Killer Cells and Fertility

Natural Killer (NK) cells are a normal part of the immune system. An overabundance of these cells in the uterus may contribute to implantation failure or miscarriage. Steroid treatment is the common Western medicine approach. Chinese herbal medicine may also support management. Kim recommends a combined holistic and medical approach.

### Fragmented Embryos

Fragmented embryos arise during IVF when an embryo begins to divide but fragments before the five-day transfer point. Research suggests Chinese medicine, including acupuncture, herbs, diet, and lifestyle changes, may positively influence embryo quality and endometrial thickness. Kim recommends addressing all aspects of general health to support egg and embryo quality.

### Low Ovarian Reserve and Egg Quality

Low ovarian reserve means fewer eggs remain. While the number cannot be increased, egg quality can be supported. Kim's approach includes an organic plant-based diet, prenatal vitamins, CoQ10 supplementation, fish oil, Chinese herbal medicine to strengthen Qi, quality sleep, stress reduction, exercise, and Vitamin D optimisation. Chinese herbal formulas are tailored to the individual.

### PCOS and PMOS

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) has been renamed PMOS (Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome) to better reflect the multisystem nature of the condition. PMOS involves metabolic, hormonal, reproductive, psychological, and dermatological factors. Approximately 170 million people worldwide are affected. Kim provides integrative management of PMOS.

### Fertility Tests Kim Recommends

#### Anti-Mullerian Hormone (AMH)

AMH is a marker for ovarian reserve. It is not covered by Medicare and costs approximately $80. Higher results can be associated with PCOS. Kim has observed results improve with lifestyle changes.

#### Hormone Panel

Includes oestrogen, LH, FSH, SHBG, testosterone, progesterone, and DHEA. LH and FSH are best tested on day 2 of the cycle. Progesterone is best tested on day 21 of a regular cycle to confirm ovulation.

#### Sperm Testing

Infertility is approximately 50% male factor. A semen analysis covers motility, morphology, count, volume, and signs of infection. Most sperm issues can be treated. Kim applies higher standards than standard reference ranges when guiding treatment.

#### Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH)

Thyroid hormones influence the menstrual cycle. Hypothyroidism is linked to miscarriage and difficulty conceiving. Kim prefers TSH between 1 and 2, and below 2.5 when trying to conceive.

#### Vitamin D

Low vitamin D is common in Australia and has been linked to PCOS, endometriosis, and low AMH. Kim prefers results over 120 for fertility purposes.

#### MTHFR Gene Test

MTHFR gene variants affect folate processing. A defect is linked to infertility, recurrent miscarriage, blood clotting disorders, and mood disorders. Kim recommends testing for anyone with unexplained infertility or recurrent pregnancy loss.
