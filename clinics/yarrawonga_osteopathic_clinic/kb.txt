# Yarrawonga Osteopathic Clinic

## About the Clinic
### Overview
The Yarrawonga Osteopathic Clinic has been known and trusted by locals for over 20 years. The clinic focuses on improving body function, mobility and flexibility, from back pain to sporting related injuries.
### Practitioners
Under the careful guidance of Dr. Frank O’Dwyer, our practitioners work with you, using a range of techniques and procedures. Our practitioners are fully qualified and undergo constant professional development.

## Conditions Treated
### What We Treat
We treat a range of ailments and issues at the clinic. Here are just a few of the things we can help you with: back and neck pain, sciatica, headaches & migraines, muscle strain, injuries, tendinitis, arthritis, work-related and repetitive strain injuries, and sporting related injuries.
