# Cath Lilburn — Naturopathic Herbalist

## About Cath Lilburn

Cath Lilburn is a Naturopathic Herbalist with over 25 years of clinical experience working at the intersection of traditional herbal wisdom and evidence-based practice. She specialises in supporting women in midlife and beyond, with a particular focus on perimenopause and menopause, stress and burnout, and recovery through significant health events such as breast cancer.

Cath trained under Dorothy Hall, one of Australia's most respected traditional herbalists. She later completed a Bachelor of Medicines Management with Professional Honours in Complementary Medicine through the University of Tasmania. She undertakes ongoing specialist women's health mentoring in perimenopause and menopause, and advanced microbiome and gastrointestinal training with Dr Jason Hawrelak.

Her published research on medicinal mushrooms as a potential adjunct treatment for breast cancer patients was recognised by the Naturopaths and Herbalists Association of Australia in 2021 and was a finalist for article of the year. In 2024, she became an Australian Ambassador for the Integrative and Naturopathic Medicine World Congress.

## Perimenopause and Menopause

### What is Perimenopause

Perimenopause is the 5 to 8 years leading up to menopause, which is defined as 12 months after the last menstrual period. It is a time of hormonal transition that can greatly impact mood and the nervous system.

### Perimenopause Symptoms

Symptoms during this time can include mood changes, menstrual changes and irregularities, sleep disruption, headaches and migraines, energy shifts and weight gain.

### How Cath Helps with Perimenopause

Natural therapies including herbal tonics, targeted nutrients and lifestyle adjustments can genuinely support women through this transition, easing symptoms while supporting long-term wellbeing.

Cath can specifically help with sleep and nervous system changes, mood, anxiety and emotional resilience, changing cycles and premenstrual symptoms, fatigue, weight and metabolic changes, digestive and microbiome health, bone, cardiovascular and healthy ageing foundations, and working collaboratively with a GP when Menopausal Hormone Therapy or further medical investigation may be appropriate.

## Breast Cancer Support

### Overview

Cath has a particular interest and depth of experience in supporting women through breast cancer from diagnosis, through treatment and into recovery. This includes supporting nutrition, resilience and recovery during treatment with all recommendations considered alongside oncology care, managing treatment side effects naturopathically and safely alongside oncology care, rebuilding strength, energy and resilience once active treatment ends, and longer-term support for recovery, resilience and overall health and vitality.

Physical symptoms such as post-cancer fatigue are very common following treatment, and many women feel the emotional impact of a cancer diagnosis linger after completing treatment. Individualised herbal tonics, lifestyle adjustments and focused nutritional support are helpful in this space.

### Training and Background

Cath's background in breast cancer support began in 2000 when she studied cancer support strategies with Herbalist Nancy Evelyn in Newcastle. In 2018, she completed further integrative oncology study with American Naturopath Lise Alschuler. In 2021, her University of Tasmania research on medicinal mushrooms as a potential adjunct treatment for breast cancer patients was published in the NHAA Journal and was a finalist for article of the year. She has a sub-speciality in the therapeutic use of medicinal mushrooms, including Reishi, for immune health and breast cancer recovery.

### Support During Diagnosis Shock

The period immediately after diagnosis involves absorbing frightening news while making significant decisions about surgery and treatment. Common experiences include difficulty thinking clearly, disrupted sleep, overwhelming anxiety and a sense that everything is happening too fast. Nervous system support helps steady thinking, improve sleep and build resilience through appointments and decisions.

### Support During Surgery and Oncology Treatment

During surgery, chemotherapy and radiation, support can help with preparing the body for surgery, managing treatment side effects such as nausea, fatigue and skin sensitivity, supporting immune resilience, and steadying anxiety and sleep. The aim is to work alongside the oncology team, not replace it.

### Support After Treatment Ends

Many women find themselves without a clear map for what comes next after active treatment ends. Ongoing fatigue, brain fog, disrupted sleep, anxiety about recurrence and a body that feels unfamiliar are all common. Naturopathic support focuses on rebuilding energy through targeted nutritional support and individualised herbal tonics, supporting immune resilience including through medicinal mushrooms such as Reishi, steadying the nervous system through herbal medicine, nutrition and lifestyle changes, and restoring a sense of agency through a structured recovery plan.
