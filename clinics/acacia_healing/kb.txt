# Acacia Healing

## About

Acacia Healing is a holistic wellness clinic located at 14B Lyall Street, Westbury, Tasmania (at the rear of the Post Office building). ### Lymphatic Drainage Massage

Therapist: Manon.

Manual Lymphatic Drainage (MLD) is a gentle, rhythmic massage technique that supports the natural movement of lymph fluid through the body. It helps reduce swelling, supports detoxification, enhances immune function, and promotes deep relaxation. MLD is intended as a gentle refresh for an otherwise healthy lymphatic system.

This treatment may benefit people recovering from surgery, experiencing fluid retention, reducing inflammation, supporting immune health, or seeking general wellbeing and relaxation.

#### Medical Clearance Requirement

A current medical clearance from a doctor is required before receiving this treatment, even for clients who have previously had clearance. Lymphatic drainage is not suitable for individuals with kidney disease or renal failure, congestive heart conditions, diabetes, malignant cancers, skin infections such as cellulitis or ulcers, thrombosis or blood clots, or lymphoedema (or suspected lymphoedema).

Clients experiencing persistent swelling, warmth at a site, skin discolouration, thickening, or pitting should speak to their doctor before booking, as these may be signs of lymphoedema.

#### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Do I Need Medical Clearance To Book Lymphatic Drainage

Yes. A current medical clearance from your doctor is required before any lymphatic drainage session. This ensures the treatment is safe for your specific health needs.

#### Is Lymphatic Drainage Painful

No. MLD is a very light, flowing treatment. The pressure is soft and gentle, which is appropriate for the lymphatic system that sits close to the surface of the skin.

#### How Many Lymphatic Drainage Sessions Will I Need

This depends on individual needs. Some clients find a single session helpful for general wellness while others may benefit from a series post-surgery or to manage fluid retention. Manon can guide you at your initial appointment.

#### Can I Book Lymphatic Drainage Without A Medical Condition

Yes. Lymphatic drainage can support general wellness, immune health, and relaxation. It is also popular during seasonal changes or times of physical or emotional stress.

## Structural Integration Session Preparation

### What To Wear

Clients should wear clothing that allows for postural assessment and treatment. Options include shorts and a singlet, swimmers, or gym clothes. Avoid sports bras that cover large sections of the back. If comfortable, a postural assessment in underwear is ideal.

### On The Day

Avoid wearing moisturiser or artificial fragrances on the day of a session. Oil-based products can interfere with myofascial release work, and Chris has multiple chemical sensitivities. Do not wear perfume, cologne, or body spray.

### Hydration

Drinking 2 to 4 litres of non-caffeinated, non-alcoholic fluids per day is ideal, especially before and after a session. Adequate hydration significantly improves treatment outcomes.

### Arrival

A dedicated waiting room is available. Clients are welcome to arrive a little early. There are five stairs to access the clinic.

### What To Expect

Sessions begin with a postural assessment to identify compensation patterns. This is an active treatment where clients move body parts while being worked on. Sessions run up to 90 minutes for follow-ups and up to 120 minutes for initial consultations. If the desired change is achieved earlier, the session may conclude sooner to allow time for integration.

### Between Sessions

Stand and move every hour where possible. Vary how you sit, stand, and drive. Do not force correct posture. A warm bath with Epsom salts after a session can feel restorative. If anything feels sharp, severe, or persists beyond a few days, contact Chris.
