## Physiotherapy

### Treatment Approach
Following an initial assessment, where any injuries or concerns are identified, we work with you to provide a goal based treatment plan. Treatments include evidence based hands on therapy including massage, dry needling and manual therapy, as well as focused exercise prescription with an emphasis on mobility and strength. We focus on education around your injury and treat the underlying cause of any issue, not just the symptoms.

### Conditions Treated
We specialise in treating a wide range of conditions such as spinal injuries, sports injuries, upper limb injuries (shoulder, elbow, wrist and hand), lower limb injuries (hip, knee and ankle), postural or biomechanical issues, running injuries, chronic pain, post operative rehabilitation, headaches, and falls prevention. We also see patients without injuries who want to get stronger, improve performance or prevent injuries.

### Treatment Techniques

#### Dry Needling
Dry needling is a therapeutic technique that targets trigger points in muscles, helping to relieve pain and improve mobility. It promotes blood flow to the injured area, facilitating healing and reducing muscle tension.

#### Manual Therapy
Manual therapy helps reduce pain and muscle tension, increases flexibility and promotes better circulation. Manual therapy, including sports massage, can improve joint mobility and function.

#### Exercise Rehabilitation
Exercise rehabilitation improves performance, reduces pain, accelerates recovery and strengthens muscles. Pilates is used to enhance strength and rehabilitate the core, promoting stability and flexibility.

### Referrals, Medicare and Private Health Insurance
#### Do I need a referral for Physio?
No, you do not need a referral from a GP to attend physio. However, you can get an EPC plan from your GP which entitles you to 5 subsidised physio visits per calendar year from Medicare.

#### Do we accept private health insurance?
Yes, we accept all health insurance providers on the day via HICAPS.

#### Can we refer for Imaging such as X-Ray or MRI?
Yes, we can. However, in most cases Medicare will not cover a rebate if the referral is from a physio. X-ray: we can refer and you will receive a Medicare rebate. MRI: requires a GP referral to receive any Medicare or private health rebate. Ultrasound: we can refer for some conditions such as suspected muscular strains.
