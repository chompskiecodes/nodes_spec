# One Stop Rehab

## Overview
### About One Stop Rehab
One Stop Rehab offers evidence-based practice and long-term recovery. Whether you want to improve performance, recover from injuries, or return to sport, the team can help. The team focuses on long term healing instead of a quick fix, treating the cause rather than the symptoms. Each patient gets a personalised plan developed with them, not a one size fits all plan. From chronic pain to neuro to spinal to sports, the team has specialised expertise to address a wide range of conditions. Services include physiotherapy, group classes, hydrotherapy, and gym-based exercise programs.
### Services Offered
Pre and post-op rehabilitation, clinical reformer pilates, sports physiotherapy, dry needling, women's health physiotherapy, hypermobility conditions, strength sessions, PD Warrior program, and the ONERO osteoporosis and osteopenia program. Exercise physiology is also available. The online enquiry form lists these services: PD Warrior, Exercise Physiology, Physiotherapy, Clinical Reformer Pilates, ONERO program, and Other.

## Services
### Sports Physiotherapy
The team has experience working with athletes from a range of sports including swimming, rugby league (NRL), rugby union, soccer, netball, and ultimate frisbee. Sports physiotherapy is inclusive and tailored to athletes across age groups, and includes injury management, injury prevention, strength and conditioning protocols and training plans to allow for optimal return to sport.
#### Who is it for
Everyone who plays sports including gym goers.
#### Benefits
Improve performance in sport or gym, less injuries and quicker return.
### Strength Sessions
Strength sessions are exercise scientist-led programs designed to improve strength, confidence, movement quality, and overall function. Each session is tailored to your goals, whether you are recovering from injury, managing a chronic condition, returning to sport, or wanting to build strength safely. The focus is on correct technique, progressive loading, and long-term results.
#### Who is it for
People recovering from injury or surgery, managing chronic pain or ongoing conditions, returning to sport or gym, or wanting a structured and supervised strength program.
### Clinical Reformer Pilates
Clinical reformer pilates is a physiotherapist-led exercise program using a specialised reformer machine to improve strength, flexibility, posture, and movement control. Unlike generic pilates classes, it is tailored to your specific needs, often for rehabilitation, injury prevention, or performance enhancement. Exercises are low-impact but highly targeted, making them suitable for all levels, from beginners to athletes.
#### Who is it for
People recovering from injury or surgery, or managing chronic conditions.
#### Benefits
Strengthens core and stabilising muscles, improves posture and alignment, supports injury prevention and rehabilitation.
### Dry Needling
Dry needling is a targeted treatment technique used by physiotherapists to help relieve muscle tightness, pain, and movement restrictions. It involves fine, sterile needles placed into specific trigger points or tight areas of muscle to help reduce tension and improve mobility. It is often used alongside exercise, manual therapy, and rehabilitation.
#### Who is it for
People experiencing muscle tightness, trigger points, sports injuries, neck or back pain, headaches, overuse injuries, or reduced range of motion.
### Pre and Post-Operative Rehabilitation
Comprehensive pre- and post-operative rehabilitation for a wide range of surgical procedures, including spinal surgeries, ACL reconstruction, meniscus repairs, total knee and hip replacements, shoulder surgeries, and carpal tunnel treatments.
#### Who is it for
People who have undergone surgeries or have surgery dates.
#### Benefits
Pre-op improves odds of recovery post-operatively. Post-op rehab allows for full return to function and reduces complications.
### Women's Health Physiotherapy
Women's health physiotherapy provides personalised care for conditions affecting the pelvic floor, bladder, bowel, pregnancy, postnatal recovery, and pelvic pain, using evidence-based assessment and treatment.
#### Who is it for
Women experiencing pregnancy-related pain, postnatal recovery concerns, pelvic floor weakness, bladder or bowel symptoms, pelvic pain, pain with intercourse, prolapse symptoms, or those wanting guidance with safe return to exercise.
### PD Warrior Program
PD Warrior exercise programs lead the way in neuroactive exercises designed to retrain your brain and improve your Parkinson's symptoms. It is for early to late stage Parkinson's, for symptom management, neuroplasticity, confidence and mood.
### Osteoporosis and Osteopenia Program (ONERO)
An evidence-based program called Onero that provides weight bearing exercises to improve bone density. The classes include core strengthening and balance. Classes are supervised at all times by certified Onero licensees as well as certified health professionals.
#### Who is it for
Those who live with or have been recently diagnosed with osteoporosis and/or osteopenia.
#### Benefits
Improve bone density, strength and balance, reduce injury and non-traumatic fractures, and improve muscle mass.
### Hypermobility Conditions
The team has experience working with connective tissue conditions such as Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome and Cranio-Cervical Instability (CCI), using management forms such as traction, bed pilates, cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), and strengthening.
#### Who is it for
Those suffering from connective tissue conditions such as Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome and Cranio-Cervical Instability (CCI).
#### Benefits
Improve function, reduce pain, achieve goals, and a pillar of a multidisciplinary approach to treating hypermobility conditions.

## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Where are you located?
We are located in Westmead and Leonay. Give us a call on 0405 088 137 to find out more.
#### How long are the sessions?
Initial assessments are from 30 minutes to 60 minutes depending on the injury. This is to allow for ample time to assess, diagnose, educate, treat, and establish a home exercise program. Subsequent assessments are 30 minutes to allow for treatment and home exercise program updates to be made.
#### What do I bring to my first appointment?
You do not need a referral to attend physiotherapy as we are primary health providers. Please bring a doctor's referral (if applicable), any scans that you had (if applicable), and appropriate loose fitting clothing for the movement assessment. If you are under WorkCover or CTP please bring your claim number, name of the insurance company, and any other information relating to your case manager.
#### What are your fees?
We bulk bill under the EPC medicare program once a GP referral has been provided. For private patients or private health insurance holders, fees are $160 for initial appointments.
#### Do you have a cancellation policy?
We ask you to cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment by messaging 0405 088 137. If you fail to attend your appointment without notice then you may be charged a fee of $30. The cancellation fee must be paid before any further appointments are made.
#### Who can go to physiotherapy?
Anyone and everyone. Whether you are an adult, elderly, athletic, child, recovering from surgery, want pre-op rehab, looking to get fit or stay fit, or experiencing pain, physiotherapy is a service for all.
