1. What is osteopathic and chiropractic care?

Osteopathic and chiropractic care are hands-on healthcare approaches that focus on root cause diagnosis and treating musculoskeletal conditions affecting the spine, joints, muscles, and nervous system. No referral is needed as we are primary health care practitioners.

Our chiropractors provide blended treatment which may include adjustments,  myofascial release, spinal traction, dry needling acupuncture, instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilisation (IASTM), fascia release, active release, trigger point, and cupping therapy. Also we provide tailored advice on rehab exercises, posture and ergonomic to help restore movement, reduce pain, support recovery and improve nervous system function.

2. What happens during your initial consultation?

Your first appointment typically includes:

A detailed medical history review

Discussion of your symptoms and goals

Postural and movement assessment

Physical exam

Possibly imaging

Diagnosis and treatment plan

If appropriate, treatment may begin during the first visit and may include treatment modalities like adjustments, dry needle acupuncture, fascia release, active release and rehabilitation advice.

3. Are osteopathic and chiropractic adjustments safe?

Yes! Osteopathic and Chiropractic treatments are considered safe and effective when performed by qualified practitioners.

Practitioners are extensively trained undergoing a Bachelors Degree and Masters in University with evidence based research to assess whether treatment is appropriate for you. Techniques are always adapted to suit your age, medical history, comfort level and treatment goals. Most patients experience significant relief and improved mobility following treatment.

We also offer a number of alternative treatments for those who do not choose to have adjustments.

4. Do osteopathic / chiropractic  adjustments hurt?

Most patients find adjustments comfortable and relieving rather than painful.

You may feel gentle pressure or movement during an adjustment.

After treatment, you may  feel mild soreness similar to post-exercise muscle soreness, but this usually settles within 24–48 hours.

5. Will the adjustments make my bones crack?

The “cracking” sound heard during an adjustment is not bones cracking.

It is caused by small gas bubbles releasing within the joint fluid as pressure changes — similar to clicking your knuckles. This is completely normal and painless.

Not all treatments involve “cracking” and we can employ many other techniques that are more comfortable if preferred.

6. How many treatments will I need?

The number of treatments varies depending on:

Your condition

How long the problem has been present

Your activity levels and lifestyle

Your treatment goals

Most of our patients feel experience noticeable relief in the first visit while others benefit from a course of care to fully resolve the issue and prevent recurrence.

In general, the sooner you come in, the faster you will heal.

Your practitioner will discuss realistic expectations during your consultation.

7. Is osteopathic/chiropractic care only for back and neck pain?

No! While Chiropractors commonly treat back and neck pain, we can also help with many musculoskeletal issues including:

Headaches, Migraines and TMJ pain

Postural problems

Shoulder and hip pain

Elbow and Knee pain

Joint stiffness and mobility issues

8. Can osteopathic/chiropractic care improve posture?

Yes! Poor posture can place stress on the spine, muscles, and joints.

Our Treatment help by:

Improving spinal mobility

Reducing muscle tension

Correcting movement patterns

Providing posture and ergonomic advice

Prescribing strengthening exercises

Improving posture can help reduce strain and prevent recurring pain.

9. Do you prescribe exercises or rehabilitation?

Yes. Exercise and rehabilitation make up an integral part of our treatment. We aim to find the root cause and provide the relevant strengthening steps to reduce the risk of re-injury and promote lasting results and not band-aid solutions.

Your practitioner may recommend specific exercises to:

Improve strength and stability

Increase mobility

support spinal health

prevent future injuries

These exercises are tailored to your individual condition and goals.

10. Can children or pregnant women come for Osteopathic/Chiropractic care?

Yes. Osteopathic / Chiropractic care can be adapted for all ages, including children and pregnant women.

Practitioners use gentle, modified techniques designed to be safe and comfortable. Many people seek care during pregnancy for back pain, pelvic discomfort, and posture changes.

11. Is treatment covered by private health insurance and do I need a referral?

In most cases, private health insurance with extras cover includes osteopathic and chiropractic treatment. It’s always best to check your level of cover with your health fund.

OSTEOPATHY + CHIROPRACTIC

Osteopathy and Chiropractic are forms of manual medicine focusing on the relationship between structure and function of the body. It a form of manual therapy that was developed  in the Victorian Era in the 19th Century.

Osteopathy is derived from the ancient Greek word   Osteon  “bone” and  pathos  “suffering, disease”. Chiropractic is derived from the ancient Greek word  cheir  “hand” and  praktos  “done” = “done by hand”.

Research suggests osteopathic and chiropractic care, including spinal adjustments, may help reduce the frequency, intensity and duration of migraines, while also lowering reliance on pain medication.

Evidence also supports spinal manipulation for chronic back pain, with improvements in both pain and function compared to standard care.

These hands-on, non-invasive treatments help reduce back and neck pain, improve movement and relieve muscle tension — without relying on medication.

By improving joint mobility and reducing strain on the body, treatment can help you feel more comfortable in daily activities and support long-term recovery.
