Who I Work With

Many people who arrive at KYND Psychology have already tried to make sense of things on their own. They are thoughtful, capable, often highly self-aware, yet something keeps returning them to the same inner conflict.

Some are navigating  anxiety  and  depression , where life continues outwardly but feels heavier underneath.

Others are living with the effects of  complex trauma  or  PTSD , where the past continues to shape the present in ways that are difficult to name.

Some struggle with  low self-esteem ,  identity issues , or a  persistent sense of not knowing who they are .

Others feel caught in  relationship  and  attachment difficulties , repeating painful patterns with partners, family, or within themselves.

Some come in the midst of  grief and loss , while others are facing major life transitions such as separation, migration, career change, or questions of meaning.

Others seek help for  emotional regulation difficulties ,  chronic stress , or  inner pressure  that has become exhausting to carry alone.

Some come with diagnoses such as  Borderline Personality Disorder  and are looking for more than symptom management, seeking deeper and more lasting change.

What they share is a readiness to look honestly at their inner world, and a desire for something more than coping.

If that sounds like you, this may be the right space. You can explore our  clinical psychology and psychotherapy services in Kew  to learn more about our treatment approaches, session fees, and Medicare rebates.

Navid’s therapeutic approach integrates classical psychoanalytic depth with a compassionate, contemporary perspective. He provides a secure and reflective environment where individuals and couples can explore their inner worlds, uncover unconscious patterns, and foster profound, lasting change.

Something More Than Coping

Therapy here is not about quick fixes or surface-level relief. It is about understanding yourself at a level that actually changes things, slowly, honestly, and with someone who is genuinely paying attention.

A Space to Think

Most of us rarely have a place where we can speak without editing ourselves, without performing, without rushing toward a conclusion. Therapy offers exactly that. A dedicated space to slow down, follow the thread, and listen to what has been quietly asking to be heard.

Beneath the Surface

This work goes beyond managing symptoms. Drawing on psychoanalytic thinking, the focus is on what lies beneath: the unconscious conflicts, identity struggles, and relational patterns that shape who we are long before we are aware of them.

How We Are Shaped

We are not formed in isolation. The relationships that shaped us early in life continue to live inside us, showing up in how we love, how we argue, how we withdraw. This approach takes those patterns seriously, not to assign blame, but to understand and, where possible, to change them.

Funding Options

Referrals

A referral is not required to book an appointment at KYND Psychology. Clients can attend privately without a referral.

A valid referral and Mental Health Treatment Plan are only required if you wish to claim Medicare rebates.

Medicare Rebates

Clients referred by a GP or psychiatrist under a valid Mental Health Care Plan may be eligible to receive Medicare rebates for psychological treatment.

As sessions are provided by a  Clinical Psychologist , the current Medicare rebate is  $149.05 per session . Eligible clients may access rebates for up to  10 sessions per calendar year , subject to Medicare requirements.

Please note that Couples Therapy sessions are not eligible for Medicare rebates.

Private Health Insurance

If your extras cover includes psychology, you may be able to claim a rebate through your private health insurer. Eligibility and rebate amounts vary between providers and levels of cover, so please check directly with your insurer.

Please note that the same session cannot be claimed through both Medicare and private health insurance.

Session Fees

KYND Psychology is a private practice and does not offer bulk billing.

Appointments are 50 minutes in duration. Payment is due on the day of your appointment unless alternative arrangements have been made in advance.

After-hours appointments attract a higher consultation fee.

For current fees, please contact  info@kyndpsychology.com.au .

Cancellations and Missed Appointments

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, we kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice.

Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or unattended appointments, incur the full consultation fee, as the appointment time has been reserved specifically for you. Medicare rebates are not available for cancellation fees.

Enquiries

If you have any questions about appointments, current fees, Medicare rebates, or the most suitable service for your needs, please feel free to contact us at  info@kyndpsychology.com.au . We are happy to assist you.

Welcome to my therapy practice. I am a Clinical Psychologist and Psychoanalytic Psychotherapist who has spent years doing one thing with genuine commitment: providing a deep, reflective space, and helping people understand themselves more honestly than they could alone.

Approach

My clinical work is grounded in
classical and contemporary psychoanalytic theory
, specifically the traditions of
Freud and Lacan
. While psychoanalysis forms the foundation of my practice, I integrate evidence-based approaches when clinically appropriate, including
Transference-Focused Psychotherapy (TFP), Mentalization-Based Therapy (MBT)
, and contemporary frameworks like ACT and CBT to suit each client’s unique needs.

Values

Depth, clarity, and the therapeutic alliance
are at the core of my practice. I believe in a sustained commitment to understanding the "how" and "why" behind emotional pain, creating a collaborative space where meaningful and lasting psychological change can unfold.
