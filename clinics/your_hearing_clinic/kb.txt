## Hearing Tests
### About Hearing Tests
A private hearing test is simple, comfortable and non-invasive. It is longer and more detailed than an NHS test and looks at how you hear in real-life situations. A full hearing assessment usually takes around 45 to 60 minutes. Adults should be tested every 1 to 2 years, especially over 50, or sooner if sounds are muffled, there is tinnitus or difficulty hearing in background noise. Children from age 3 can be assessed with age-appropriate techniques by GP or ENT referral.
### What Happens
The audiologist talks through your hearing concerns and daily listening needs, completes a medical questionnaire, looks into your ears with a video otoscope, carries out a hearing test in a sound-treated room plus speech testing if needed, explains the results in everyday language and discusses options. Where warranted there is an onward referral and report to your GP or ENT.
### Hearing Screener
The website has an online hearing screener that identifies specific tones you may have difficulty hearing and gauges whether a diagnostic hearing test would suit you. Not all frequencies are tested.

## Hearing Aid Assessment and Fitting
### Assessment
A comprehensive hearing aid assessment, usually 90 to 120 minutes for the first appointment, includes a medical and lifestyle questionnaire, examination of the ears including video otoscopy, ear pressure examination, speech testing including speech in noise testing, a thorough explanation of results, a hearing loss simulation and hearing aid demonstration if applicable, advice on technology and styles, and clear pricing. Any wax can be removed before testing if needed.
### Fitting Process
Fitting may be the same day or a later appointment depending on whether devices are available or need ordering. Behind the ear and receiver in canal models can often be fitted at the second appointment within 1 to 2 weeks. Custom moulded devices need impressions and take about 1 to 2 weeks to be ready. At the fitting the audiologist programs the aids, and you learn insertion, removal, cleaning, maintenance and charging. Follow-up appointments are typically at 2 weeks and 6 weeks after fitting, and further appointments continue until you are satisfied.
### Trials and Warranty
Hearing aid trials are offered onsite with a recommended device. The clinic offers 90 days to decide whether the hearing aids are benefitting you. Hearing aids come with a 4 year warranty. Most hearing aids last 5 to 7 years.
### Aftercare
Aftercare is included for the life of the hearing aids, including follow-up appointments, adjustments, repairs, servicing, yearly reviews, annual hearing tests and wax removal (depending on the package chosen). Loan devices are available for Your Hearing Clinic clients if hearing aids need to go for repair.
### Brands

### Hearing Aid Styles
#### IIC Invisible in Canal
Worn deep in the ear canal and near invisible, depending on the size and shape of the ear canal. Suitable for mild to moderate hearing losses.
#### CIC Completely in Canal
A small, discreet device that fits inside the ear canal with a customised fit. Most suitable for mild to moderate hearing losses.
#### ITC In the Canal or In the Ear
For clients who want more technology or have dexterity issues. Can be rechargeable with Bluetooth for phone connection. Suitable for mild to severe hearing losses.

Sits behind the ear with a tube directing sound into the ear canal, with adjustable volume controls and multiple listening programs. Popular for severe to profound hearing loss.

A 100 percent invisible hearing aid worn continuously for months at a time, placed deep in the ear canal with no daily handling or battery changes. ## Ear Wax Removal
### About Microsuction
Wax removal is done by a qualified audiologist using microsuction and gentle irrigation (instrumentation can also be used), with the method chosen after examining the ear. Microsuction uses a small medical suction device while looking directly into the ear canal, with no high pressure water syringing. It is safe for sensitive ears and for people with a history of perforated eardrums, because it uses no water. Most appointments take 10 to 20 minutes.
### What to Expect
The ears are examined with a video otoscope, showing the wax before and after removal. The audiologist explains the best method, removes the wax and checks hearing improvement afterwards. No referral is needed. Same-day or next-day appointments are usually available, with slots kept open for urgent cases.
### Symptoms of Wax Build-Up
Muffled or reduced hearing, a feeling of blockage or pressure, ringing (tinnitus), itchiness or irritation, and difficulty with hearing aids.
### How Often
For most people once a year, but more often if you naturally produce more wax or use hearing aids. A gentle maintenance clean every 6 to 12 months works well for those prone to wax.

## Ear Impressions
Appointments include a detailed medical history of your ears and hearing, examination of your ears under a microscope and an explanation of ear anatomy. A cotton stop is placed in the ear canal followed by impression material, which feels cold and as though you are under water, and sets in a few minutes. Wax removal can be done in the same appointment if required. Custom hearing protection is available for musicians, DJs and concert-goers.

## Brain Hearing Programme
The Brain Hearing Programme, Listening and Communication Enhancement (LACE AI Pro), is an app-based auditory training programme that improves how your brain processes speech, particularly in noisy environments. It suits people who wear hearing aids, have mild hearing loss or have been told their hearing is normal but struggle to follow conversations. It uses speech in noise training, rapid speech exercises, working memory tasks and cognitive listening activities. Fifteen minutes of training a day for 30 days can significantly help understanding of speech in noise. In clinical trials over 80 percent of patients showed improvement, over 25 percent showed better understanding of speech in noise, many noticed a difference in 2 to 4 weeks and improvements lasted over 6 months. Lifetime access costs £275.

### Hearing Tests
#### What happens during a hearing test?
The audiologist discusses your hearing and the situations you find harder, with a detailed questionnaire. Then the outer ear and eardrum are checked, followed by a series of listening tests. Results are explained clearly with helpful next steps.
#### What if my hearing test shows normal hearing?
That is good news and still valuable for reassurance and baseline tracking.
#### Is a private hearing test better than one on the NHS?
The NHS test is useful but often short and focused on medical need. A private test is longer, more detailed, looks at real-life hearing and leaves time for questions and personalised options.
#### How long does a full hearing test take?
Around 45 to 60 minutes.
#### Do I need a hearing test or is my hearing loss just caused by earwax?
Earwax can cause muffled hearing but is not the only reason hearing changes. The clinic checks both wax and hearing levels in the same appointment.
#### How do I know if my hearing loss is age-related or something else?
Age-related loss usually happens gradually, with speech sounding softer or less clear especially in background noise. Sudden changes, or changes in only one ear, should be checked urgently.
#### Should I be concerned if only one ear is affected?
Hearing changes or tinnitus in just one ear are worth checking sooner. Often it is wax, but occasionally it can be related to the auditory nerve, so the clinic does thorough diagnostic testing and refers to your GP or specialist ENT where required.
#### Why do I struggle hearing in groups or busy places?
This is one of the first signs of age-related hearing change. The brain has to work harder to separate speech from background noise, and a hearing test shows how well you process speech.
#### Can loud concerts or earphones cause permanent hearing loss?
Yes, prolonged loud sound can damage the inner ear. If you have ringing or muffled hearing after events or headphone use, a hearing test is recommended.
#### Can hearing loss be treated without hearing aids?
It depends on the cause. Wax blockages or ear infections can be treated without hearing aids, but age-related and noise-induced hearing loss usually needs digital hearing technology.
#### Is hearing loss always permanent?
Not always. Wax, infection or fluid can cause temporary changes.
#### Can blocked ears affect balance?
Yes, ear problems can contribute to dizziness or imbalance.
#### Can hearing loss cause tiredness or brain fog, or affect memory?
Yes. Untreated hearing loss increases listening effort, leading to fatigue and reduced concentration, and research shows it can affect memory and cognitive load.

### Ear Wax and Tinnitus
#### Is ear wax removal by microsuction safe?
Yes, when carried out by a trained audiologist. A gentle medical suction device is used while looking directly into the ear, with no water blasting or syringing.
#### Is microsuction uncomfortable or painful?
Most people find it a strange sensation but not painful or uncomfortable. You may hear the suction noise, and it is usually very quick.
#### Is microsuction ear wax removal safer than ear syringing?
Yes, microsuction is considered the safest method, gentle and water-free under full visual guidance.
#### Can I still have microsuction if I have had a perforated eardrum?
Yes, it is usually the preferred method because it does not use water. The ear is always examined first.
#### Can wax removal help if my ears feel full after flying?
If due to wax, microsuction often helps right away. If it is middle ear pressure, the clinic checks eardrum movement and advises on safe relief.
#### How quickly can I book an ear wax removal appointment?
Most people can be seen same-day or next-day depending on availability.
#### How long does ear wax removal take?
Most appointments take 10 to 20 minutes.
#### How often should I get ear wax removed?
Once a year for most people, more often if you produce more wax or use hearing aids. The clinic refers to your GP or ENT if it detects infection or anything needing medical assessment.
#### Why do some people get ear wax more than others?
Partly genetics, partly ear shape, and sometimes linked to hearing aids or earbuds.
#### Do cotton buds help clean my ears?
No. Cotton buds push wax deeper and risk irritation or scratching the ear canal. If your ears feel blocked, have them checked.
#### What symptoms suggest I might need ear wax removal?
Muffled hearing, itching, tinnitus or hearing aids working poorly.
#### Can I have ear wax removal if I wear hearing aids?
Yes, and it is often recommended.
#### Does ear wax cause tinnitus?
Yes, it can. Once the wax is safely removed, tinnitus often reduces or goes away.
#### Do you treat tinnitus?
Yes, the clinic offers tinnitus assessments and management, including sound therapy and hearing aid solutions.
#### Can stress make tinnitus worse?
Yes. Relaxation strategies, better sleep and sound therapy often help.
#### Is tinnitus permanent?
Not always. It can improve once hearing is supported or wax is removed, and there are effective strategies to make it far less noticeable.
