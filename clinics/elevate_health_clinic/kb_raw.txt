FAQs

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly with any of our chiropractors. No GP referral is needed.

Can I claim on my private health insurance?

Yes. Chiropractic is covered under extras by most major health funds, and you can claim on the spot with HICAPS at the clinic. Your rebate depends on your fund and level of cover.

What happens at my first appointment?

Your chiropractor takes a full history, assesses how you move and explains what they find. If care is suitable, you’ll usually start treatment in the same session and leave with a clear plan.

How long are appointments?

Initial appointments are 45 minutes, or 60 minutes with Nadine. Follow-ups are 30 minutes. Nadine also offers a 45-minute follow-up with rehab, and 15-minute follow-ups for children.

Which chiropractor should I book?

Andre focuses on sports and complex back complaints, Zachary sees a wide range of everyday aches and pains, and Nadine focuses on Women’s Health and children. Not sure? Call us on
and we’ll help you choose.

Why are prices different between chiropractors?

Andre’s appointments are charged at our senior practitioner rate, reflecting his 15+ years in practice. Zachary and Nadine have their own standard rates, and Nadine’s initial adult appointment is a longer 60 minutes.

Do you see children?

Yes. Children are seen with gentle, low-force techniques adapted to their age, never the techniques used for adults. Nadine has a particular focus on children’s care.

Do you see pregnant women?

Yes. Nadine provides Chiropractic care through pregnancy and the postpartum period. See
Pregnancy & Postnatal Chiro
for more.

Will I need a scan?

Most people don’t. Your chiropractor assesses you first and only refers for imaging when it’s clinically needed.

Where are you, and is there parking?

We’re at Unit 9/22 Lexington Drive, Bella Vista, a few minutes from Norwest Business Park and about 10 minutes from Castle Hill. Free parking is available directly outside the clinic.

Pregnancy chiropractor
FAQs

No. You can book directly with Nadine. No GP referral is needed.

Is Chiropractic suitable during pregnancy?

Nadine uses low-force, pregnancy-appropriate techniques, and assessment and treatment are always adapted to your stage of pregnancy and how you’re feeling. If you have any pregnancy complications, check with your GP or midwife before booking.

Which trimester can I start?

Any. We see women from the first trimester through to the days before their due date.

Nadine takes a full history, including your pregnancy or birth, assesses how you move and explains what she finds. If care is suitable, you’ll usually start treatment in the same session and leave with a clear plan.

Initial pregnancy and postpartum appointments are 60 minutes. Follow-ups are 30 minutes, or 45 minutes with rehab.

What techniques are used?

Low-force mobilisation, soft tissue work and gentle adjustments, with positioning and cushions adapted for comfort at each stage.

How soon after birth can I come in?

As soon as you feel ready and able to travel. Many women come in within the first few weeks after birth. After a C-section, care is adapted to your recovery and progresses gently.

Can I bring my baby?

Of course. Let us know when booking so we can allow a little extra time.
