# P1 Physiotherapy

## Home

### Services

#### Physiotherapy
Thorough injury assessment and treatment planning to optimise rehabilitation. Treats musculoskeletal and sporting injuries in acute and chronic phases using soft tissue therapy, joint mobilisations, dry needling, corrective taping, postural strengthening and stretching.

#### Women's Health Physiotherapy
Treats womens health and pelvic-related issues including pregnancy, post-natal check-ups, pre-exercise screening, Pelvic Organ Prolapse (POP), incontinence, pelvic pain and pelvic floor muscle weakness. Assessments are carried out by a female physiotherapist in a dedicated private room.

#### Dry Needling
A form of Western Acupuncture where a fine, sterile stainless steel needle is inserted into a selected muscle or area to treat muscular pain and spasm, tendinopathies and myofascial dysfunction. Can also aid activation of weak muscle or reduce inflammation. Needles are individually sealed and disposed of after use.

#### Casting & Splinting
Custom waterproof, lightweight fibreglass casting for fracture management, fitted and removed on site. Custom thermo-plastic splints also available, made-to-fit and removable for cleaning. Walking boots and a range of specialised braces are supplied and fitted for injuries across the body.

#### Exercise Physiology
Individualised exercise programs to enhance recovery from workplace or sporting injury, pre- or post-surgery rehabilitation, and management of chronic conditions. The clinic has exercise equipment and a functional gym space for supervised programs.

#### Remedial Massage
Deep tissue massage to relieve muscle spasm and tightness and release pressure points, for pain and tension relief and to restore muscle length. Also assists circulation.

#### Shockwave Therapy
Used for tendinopathies, fasciopathies, soft tissue conditions and osteoarthritic conditions, including tennis/golfers elbow, patella tendinopathy, Achilles tendinopathy, gluteal tendinopathy, plantar fasciitis, heel spurs, adhesive capsulitis and osteoarthritis. Sound waves promote healing by stimulating tissue remodelling and reducing pain.

#### Post-natal Check-ups
Offered any time after giving birth, either via telehealth from hospital or face-to-face at the clinic. A comprehensive assessment at 6 weeks post-natal is recommended, especially before returning to exercise. Ante-natal classes and post-natal check-ups can be arranged by contacting the practice.

### Team

#### Michael Pasqualin
Principal Physiotherapist. B App Sci (Exe and Sport Sci), Masters (Physiotherapist).

Michael has been a qualified Physiotherapist since 2011, completing his Masters of Physiotherapy at the University of Sydney with an undergraduate degree in Exercise & Sport Science.

Over the past 10 years, Michael has treated patients of all ages with a broad range of conditions and injuries, effectively helping them to return to full function. Michael works closely with Orthopaedic and Neurological surgeons to help manage their patients' recovery after surgery. He also fits braces, boots and custom-made splints and waterproof fibreglass casts at the Oran Park location.

Michael is a dedicated hands-on Physiotherapist who tailors his treatment to each individual, using soft-tissue therapy, joint mobilisations, dry needling and exercise prescription as well as educating patients on their condition, treatment plan and self-management strategies.

Michael's clinical interests include musculoskeletal, sports and work-related injuries, pre and post surgery rehabilitation, paediatric physiotherapy and injury prevention.

Please email Michael should you have any health matters or concerns you would like to discuss at michael@p1physio.com.au.
