Always default to Australian DD/MM/YY order for a bare numeric date — the first number is the day, the second is the month (e.g. "07/11/61" → 1961-11-07, "14/3/1985" → 1985-03-14). Switch to MM/DD order only when the second number is greater than 12, since that value cannot be a month — e.g. "3/14/1985": 14 can't be a month, so 3 is the month and 14 is the day, giving 1985-03-14.
