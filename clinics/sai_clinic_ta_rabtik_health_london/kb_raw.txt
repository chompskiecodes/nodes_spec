Physiotherapy

Our Physiotherapy Services

We offer specialized physiotherapy treatments designed to reduce pain, enhance mobility,
and support recovery. Our personalized home-based care ensures that every client receives expert
treatment tailored to their specific needs, helping them regain strength and independence. With
expert physiotherapists and a holistic approach, we focus on restoring movement, flexibility,
and overall well-being. Whether recovering from an injury or managing a long-term condition, our
team is here to help right in the comfort of your home.

Sports Physiotherapy

Sports physiotherapy aids in injury prevention, rehabilitation, and
performance enhancement. It helps athletes recover and improve flexibility,
strength, and mobility, with 4-6 sessions typically needed for optimal results.

Integrated Musculoskeletal

Integrated Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy treats pain and improves
mobility with personalized care. Typically, 4-8 sessions are needed for
effective recovery and lasting results.

Ultrasound Therapy

Ultrasound Therapy uses high-frequency sound waves to reduce pain,
promote healing, and improve mobility. Typically, 4-8 sessions are needed, with
acute conditions improving faster and chronic issues requiring more treatment.

Acupuncture / Dry Needling

Acupuncture is a safe therapy that uses fine needles to stimulate
specific points in the body, promoting healing, pain relief, and energy flow.
Typically requiring 3-12 sessions for lasting benefits.

Kizhi (Herbal Poultice Massage)

Kizhi uses warm herbal poultices to target inflammation, muscle
stiffness, and joint pain. 7-14 sessions are recommended for optimal results,
especially for those recovering from injury, suffering from arthritis, or
experiencing chronic muscle discomfort.

Januvasthi (Knee Pain Relief Therapy)

Januvasthi targets knee discomfort by retaining warm medicated oil over
the joints, relieving pain, stiffness, and arthritis symptoms while enhancing
mobility. 7-15 sessions are recommended for optimal results.

Agnikarma (Pain Relief Heat Therapy)

Agnikarma uses controlled heat to relieve chronic pain, inflammation,
and joint stiffness. 5-10 sessions are recommended for optimal results, assisting in
long-term pain management and mobility restoration.

Kativasthi (Lower Back Oil Therapy)

Kativasthi uses warm herbal oil to relieve chronic lower back pain,
sciatica, and stiffness. 7-10 sessions are recommended for optimal results,
providing long-term pain management and mobility restoration.

Nasyakarma (Nasal Detox Therapy)

Nasyakarma is a nasal therapy that clears sinuses, improves respiratory
function, and enhances mental focus. 7-14 sessions are recommended for optimal
results, ideal for treating allergies, sinusitis, and migraines.

Nabhivasthi (Navel Therapy)

Nabhivasthi involves applying warm oil around the navel area to promote
digestion, relieve bloating, and balance gut health. 5-10 sessions are recommended
for optimal results, enhancing overall digestive wellness.

Lepa (Herbal Paste Therapy)

Lepa is an herbal paste application that detoxifies the skin, reduces
inflammation, and promotes healing. 5-10 sessions are recommended for optimal
results, beneficial for muscle pain, swelling, and skin conditions.

Akshitarpana (Eye Rejuvenation Therapy)

Akshitarpana involves pooling medicated ghee around the eyes, relieving
strain, dryness, and fatigue, while improving vision and overall eye health. 7-12
sessions are recommended for optimal results.

Shirodhara (Calming Oil Therapy)

Shirodhara involves gently pouring warm herbal oil over the forehead,
reducing anxiety, promoting relaxation, and improving mental clarity. 5-7 sessions
are recommended for optimal results, ideal for stress management and improving
sleep.

Abhyanga (Full Body Oil Massage)

Abhyanga is a deeply relaxing full-body warm oil massage that enhances
circulation, reduces muscle tension, and promotes detoxification. 5-10 sessions are
recommended for optimal results, improving vitality, reducing stress, and nourishing
the skin.

Shiroabhyanga (Indian Head Massage)

Shiroabhyanga is a calming scalp, neck, and shoulder massage that
improves blood circulation, alleviates headaches, and promotes mental clarity. 5-7
sessions are recommended for optimal results, perfect for stress relief, migraine
management, and nourishing the hair.
