## Therapy Services

Therapy is available in Armidale, Port Macquarie, and online across Australia. Sessions are personalised to each client's needs, using a range of evidence-based approaches.

## Psychological Assessments

Psychological assessments are available in Armidale, Port Macquarie, and online across Australia, for individuals of all ages.

## Support for Children and Adolescents

BetterSpace provides support for children, adolescents, and their families. Areas of support include stress, anxiety and low mood; friendships and relationships; school pressures and academic stress; and identity and self-esteem.

## Supervision and Mentoring for Health Professionals and Organisations

### Clinical Supervision

BetterSpace offers clinical supervision and mentoring for Provisional Psychologists, registered clinicians, support workers, and leaders, including both provisionally and fully registered psychologists. Supervision covers clinical case discussions, ethical challenges, and meeting AHPRA requirements. Supervisors are AHPRA-registered. Sessions are available in-person or online.

#### What Supervision Includes

Personalised guidance tailored to the supervisee's goals, practice setting, and experience level. Clinical case discussions covering client work and treatment planning. Reflective practice to build self-awareness and clinical reasoning. Professional development, including frameworks and resources. Ongoing connection and support.

### Mentoring for Managers and Leaders

Mentoring is available for professionals developing leadership skills. Psychologists draw on backgrounds in education, management, healthcare, government, and NGO sectors.

### Workplace Wellbeing and Organisational Psychology

The team has experience in supervision, staff development, leadership training, and mentorship, across education, government, non-government, and corporate sectors.

#### Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

Confidential EAP services provide employees with professional counselling and support for personal and work-related challenges.

#### General Supervision and Workplace Support

Psychologists help staff navigate workplace dynamics and develop resilience, supporting collaboration and accountability.

#### Management Mentoring

Mentoring programs for managers cover emotional intelligence, communication skills, and leadership strategies.

## Fees

### Standard Fees (Private/Medicare)

General Psychologist: $200. Clinical Psychologist: $245. Psychological Assessment: $245 per hour.

### NDIS Fees

NDIS rate: $252.99 (the NDIS set rate). Services can be accessed by anyone who is Plan Managed or Self Managed and has the appropriate psychology supports in their NDIS plan.

### Employer and Business (EAP) Fees

$245 plus GST per session. Under an EAP business contract, employees can access sessions with no out-of-pocket expense to themselves.

### Session Details

Therapy sessions are 50 minutes, with the remaining 10 minutes used for administration. Medicare rebates are available in many circumstances with an appropriate GP referral. Assessment durations vary depending on need and are generally not subsidised by Medicare. BetterSpace does not offer bulk billing.

### Cancellation Fees

A cancellation fee applies for late cancellations or non-attended appointments.
