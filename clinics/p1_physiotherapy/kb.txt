### Team

#### Michael Pasqualin

Principal Physiotherapist. B App Sci (Exe and Sport Sci), Masters (Physiotherapist).

Michael has been a qualified Physiotherapist since 2011, completing his Masters of Physiotherapy at the University of Sydney with an undergraduate degree in Exercise & Sport Science.

Over the past 10 years, Michael has treated patients of all ages with a broad range of conditions and injuries, effectively helping them to return to full function. Michael works closely with Orthopaedic and Neurological surgeons to help manage their patients' recovery after surgery. He also fits braces, boots and custom-made splints and waterproof fibreglass casts at our Oran Park location.

Michael is a dedicated hands-on Physiotherapist who tailors his treatment to each individual. He finds the best results for his patients by utilising a range of techniques with soft-tissue therapy, joint mobilisations, dry needling and exercise prescription as well as educating his patients on their condition, treatment plan and self-management strategies.

Michael's clinical interests include musculoskeletal, sports and work-related injuries, pre and post surgery rehabilitation, paediatric physiotherapy and injury prevention.

Over the last 10 years Michael has worked with groups including the V8 Supercars Championship Team 18 (Mark Winterbottom, Steve Richards, Scott Pye), Football Australia, and Football NSW Marconi Stallions and Mounties Wanderers FC.

Please email Michael should you have any health matters or concerns you would like to discuss at michael@p1physio.com.au.
